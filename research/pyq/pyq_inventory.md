## 2. The 25–26 Oct 2024 date conflict — RESOLVED

Several sites (SarkariExam, ExamCart, PW.live tables, PlacementStore) list the 2024 12th-level exam as **23–26 Oct 2024**. That was the **original** schedule in Advt 11/2024 (29 Aug 2024). JagranJosh (25 Sep 2024) reports the dates were **revised to 22–24 Oct 2024** ("Previously, the exam was slated for October 25 and 26"), and its live analysis of 24–25 Oct 2024 records the exam as concluded after Day 3 (24 Oct). CareerPower, ShikshaNagari solved papers (22/23 Oct phases) and the JagranJosh question-paper article all agree on **22, 23, 24 Oct 2024, 6 shifts**. **No exam was held on 25 or 26 Oct 2024.** The 23–26 Oct listings are stale copies of the pre-revision schedule — do not use them.

## 5. Data-quality flags (questions read but EXCLUDED from the dataset)

1. **2024-P1 Q31** (female graduates problem) — ShikshaNagari prints answer "(D) 2133⅓ cm", which is plainly the answer to a different (geometry) question pasted in error. Correct arithmetic gives 440 → (C). Excluded rather than silently corrected.
2. **2023-11Feb-S2 Q16** (total internal reflection) — Examzy's "Right Answer – C" (angle of incidence > 90°) is physically wrong; none of the four options states the correct condition (incidence > critical angle). Excluded; needs the official key to resolve.
3. **2024-P1 Q138** — deleted by RSSB per ShikshaNagari's own note ("all questions except Q138… Q138 has been removed by RSSB"). Not in the sample anyway (sample is Q1–30).
4. **2024-P4 Q6** (Paralympian "two gold medals in Paralympics 2024" → Devendra Jhajharia) — included exactly as the published key gives it (D); the wording is questionable (Jhajharia won one gold in Paris 2024), flag for re-check against the official final key.
5. **Unverified weightage table:** Adda247 publishes a 2026 subject-wise split (Rajasthan GK 50, India Geo 10, Science 15, Reasoning+Maths 15, Hindi+English 15, Computer 5, CA+Public Health 10 = 120). No official source is cited for it and the 2026 advt does not publish per-subject counts in any source found. **Do not use as official weightage.**
