## Expected Value (EV) — kab guess karna hai?
| Situation | Probability | EV |
|---|---|---|
| Random guess (koi option eliminate nahi) | 25% | **0.00** |
| **1 option eliminate** karke guess | 33% | **+0.22** |
| **2 options eliminate** karke guess | 50% | **+0.67** |
| Pakka pata hai | ~100% | **+2.00** |
| Khali chhodna | — | **−0.67** |

**Score formula:** Score = (Correct × 2) − (Incorrect × 2/3) − (Blank × 2/3); E = 0.
**Worked example:** 70 sahi / 30 galat / 20 E → 140 − 20 − 0 = **120.0**. Wahi 20 khali chhode to 140 − 20 − 13.3 = **106.7 AUR disqualified** (20 > 12).
**Niyam:** Kabhi khali mat chhodo — har skip par **E** dark karo. 1 option eliminate kar sake to guess karo (EV +0.22). 2 eliminate kar sake to zaroor guess karo (EV +0.67).
