## 1. Q114 — Wrong key: two identical correct options (duplicate options)

- **Paper 1, Q114** — "Which district is Junagarh Fort located in?"
- **Options as printed:** (A) Bikaner, (B) Bikaner, (C) Udaipur, (D) Kota. Key: **A**.
- **What is wrong:** Options (A) and (B) are character-for-character identical, and both are correct — Junagarh Fort is in Bikaner. There is no single correct option; a candidate picking (B) is marked wrong for the same answer as (A).
- **Correct answer/fact:** Bikaner. Either (A) or (B) should be accepted, or one of them must be replaced (e.g. with Jodhpur or Jaipur) before use.
- **Evidence/reasoning:** Junagarh Fort is in Bikaner city, Rajasthan; it is the unconquered fort built by Raja Rai Singh. The duplication is visible in the question text itself (lines for Q114), not a matter of external fact.

## 2. Q10 — Ambiguous: two options are both pronouns in the sentence

- **Paper 1, Q10** — "'जो पढ़ेगा, वह सफल होगा।' — सर्वनाम है —" Key: **D** (जो, संबंधवाचक सर्वनाम).
- **Options:** (A) सफल (B) पढ़ेगा (C) वह (पुरुषवाचक) (D) जो (संबंधवाचक सर्वनाम).
- **What is wrong:** As worded ("सर्वनाम है" — "is the pronoun"), both (C) वह and (D) जो are pronouns occurring in the sentence. वह is a pronoun (in this correlative construction it is the correlative pronoun paired with जो); जो is the relative (संबंधवाचक) pronoun. The intended distinction (which *kind* of pronoun जो is) is not what the stem asks.
- **Correct answer/fact:** जो is indeed a संबंधवाचक (relative) pronoun, so D is the intended answer — but C is not a wrong option under the stem as written. The stem must be reworded, e.g. "'जो' किस प्रकार का सर्वनाम है?" ("What kind of pronoun is 'जो'?"), to make D uniquely correct.
- **Evidence/reasoning:** Standard Hindi grammar: जो = संबंधवाचक सर्वनाम; वह = सर्वनाम (पुरुषवाचक/निश्चयवाचक in other classifications). Both words in the sentence belong to the pronoun class, so the question as printed has two defensible answers.

## 3. Q81 — Ambiguous: "highest peak in India" (Kangchenjunga vs K2)

- **Paper 1, Q81** — "Which is the highest peak in India?" Key: **B** (Kangchenjunga, with the parenthetical "K2 is in PoK, claimed").
- **What is wrong:** The stem is ambiguous as written. K2 (8,611 m) is higher than Kangchenjunga (8,586 m) and lies in Pakistan-occupied Kashmir, which India claims as its territory; Kangchenjunga is the highest peak within India's undisputed/controlled territory. The option's parenthetical tries to rescue the ambiguity, but a candidate answering on the claimed-territory reading could legitimately argue for K2, and K2 is not even offered as an option.
- **Correct answer/fact:** Kangchenjunga (8,586 m, Sikkim) is the standard textbook answer for "highest peak in India" and is the intended key. To make it unambiguous the stem should say "highest peak within India's undisputed territory" (or "highest peak located in India").
- **Evidence/reasoning:** Heights: K2 8,611 m > Kangchenjunga 8,586 m; K2 is in the Karakoram in PoK. Standard Indian GK convention answers Kangchenjunga, so this is an ambiguity/wording defect, not a wrong key — counted as ambiguous, not as wrong key.
