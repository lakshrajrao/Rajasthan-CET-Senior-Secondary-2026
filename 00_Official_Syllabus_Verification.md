## Syllabus cross-check (pp.16–22) — all 13 heads MATCH

1. **History of Rajasthan:** major ancient civilisations & archaeological sites; prominent rulers & achievements; Revolt of 1857, peasant movements, tribal movements, Praja Mandal movements, Integration of Rajasthan; prominent historical personalities.
2. **Art & Culture of Rajasthan:** architecture & painting; folk music, musical instruments, dance & theatre; major religious sects & folk deities; social life (dresses, ornaments, fairs, festivals, customs, traditions); language, dialects & literature; prominent personalities in art & culture.
3. **Geography of India:** physical features (mountains, plateaus, deserts, plains); major rivers, dams, lakes & oceans; wildlife & sanctuaries; disaster management & climate change.
4. **Geography of Rajasthan:** major physical features; climatic conditions; natural vegetation; major soils; rivers, dams & lakes; natural resources (mineral, forest, water); livestock; wildlife, sanctuaries & conservation; population (distribution, growth, density, literacy, sex ratio); major tribes; tourism.
5. **Indian Political System (special reference to Rajasthan):** nature of Constitution, Preamble, Fundamental Rights, DPSP; President, PM & Council of Ministers, Parliament, Supreme Court, Election Commission; Governor, CM, Legislative Assembly, High Court, RPSC, State Election Commission, State Information Commission, State Human Rights Commission, Chief Secretary, District Administration; local self-government & Panchayati Raj.
6. **Economy of Rajasthan:** role of industry, agriculture, animal husbandry & mineral sector; features & issues; concepts of state income & budget; handicraft industry, unemployment, drought & famine; welfare schemes & Acts, development institutions, small enterprises, financial institutions, PRI role in rural development; MGNREGA; **Viksit Bharat Guarantee for Rozgar and Aajeevika Mission – Gramin (VB-G RAM G)**.
7. **Everyday Science:** physical & chemical changes, oxidation/reduction, catalyst; metals, non-metals & important compounds; carbon & compounds, hydrocarbons, allotropes, CFC/Freons, CNG, polymers, soap & detergents; reflection of light, laws, dispersion, lenses, vision defects; space & IT, Indian space programme; genetics (terminology, Mendel's laws, chromosomes, nucleic acids, central dogma, sex determination); environmental study (ecosystem structure, biotic factors, energy flow, biogeochemical cycles); biotechnology (general info, bio-patent, new plant varieties, transgenic organisms); economic importance of animals & plants; blood groups, transfusion, Rh factor, pathogens, human health, malnutrition, human diseases — causes & cures.
8. **Logical Reasoning & Mathematics:** blood relation, number series, alphabet series, clock, calendar, LCM & HCF, average, profit & loss, percentage, SI, CI, ratio-proportion, time-speed-distance, work & time, area (triangle, circle, ellipse, rectangle, sphere, cylinder), volume (sphere, cylinder, cube, cone), graphical representation of data, code & decode, sitting arrangement, mental & analytical ability. **No trigonometry, algebra, logarithms — those are Graduation-level.**
9. **Current Affairs:** major contemporary events at state & national level (politics, economy, society, culture, technology, geography, ecology, sports); famous personalities; state & national programmes & policies; civic duties (Fundamental Duties, moral values); salient provisions of the **Rajasthan Public Examination (Measures for Prevention of Unfair Means in Recruitment) Act, 2022**.
10. **Public Health:** basics of first aid, CPR, types of drug abuse & prevention, physical & mental fitness of youth, health risks of social-media addiction & prevention.
11. **Computer Knowledge:** characteristics & applications of computers; computer organisation incl. memory (RAM, ROM), file system, input/output devices; MS Office (Word, Excel/Spreadsheet, PowerPoint). **No internet, e-mail, cyber-security, AI — those are Graduation-level.**
12. **General Hindi:** sangya, sarvanam, visheshan, avyaya, kriya; sandhi & sandhi-vichhed; samas & samas-vigrah; upsarg; pratyaya; paryayvachi; vilom; anekarthak; shabd-yugm; shabd-shuddhi; vakya-shuddhi; vakyaansh ke liye ek shabd; muhavare & lokoktiyaan; Hindi equivalents of English technical terms; office letters (aadesh, paripatra, adhisoochna, ardh-shaskiya patra, nivida, press vigyapti).
13. **General English:** tenses/sequence of tenses; active & passive voice; direct & indirect narration; articles & determiners; prepositions; translation (Hindi↔English); official & technical terms (with Hindi versions); synonyms; antonyms; one-word substitution; unseen passage comprehension; letter writing (official, demi-official, informal).

## OMR 5th-option rules (p.22) — VERIFIED

- **Rule 1:** Each question has five options A, B, C, D, E. Darken only one circle with a **BLUE BALL POINT PEN**.
- **Rule 2:** It is mandatory to fill only one of the options for each question.
- **Rule 3:** If you are not attempting a question, darken circle **E**. If none of the five circles is darkened, **one-third (1/3) part of the marks of question shall be deducted** (= −2/3, since a question carries 2 marks).
- **Rule 4:** After solving, the candidate must ascertain that one circle is darkened for each question. **Extra 10 minutes** beyond scheduled time is provided for this.
- **Rule 5:** **A candidate who has not darkened any of the five circles in more than 10% of questions shall be disqualified.** (10% of 120 = 12; so 13+ blanks = disqualified, ≤12 blanks = −2/3 each.)
