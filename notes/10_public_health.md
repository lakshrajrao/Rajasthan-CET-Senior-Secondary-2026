# 10. PUBLIC HEALTH (जन स्वास्थ्य)

## 10.1 Health — Basic Concepts
- **Swasthya (WHO paribhasha):** Kewal rog ki anupasthiti nahi — sharirik, mansik aur samajik roop se purna swasth hona.
- **Sankramak rog (Communicable):** Ek se dusre me failta hai — TB, Malaria, Dengue, Cholera, COVID-19, AIDS.
- **Asankramak (Non-communicable):** Diabetes, Cancer, Heart disease, Hypertension — jeevan-shaili se judte hain.
- **Vector-borne:** Machhar se — Malaria (Anopheles), Dengue/Chikungunya (Aedes), Kala-azar (sandfly).
- **Jal-janit (Water-borne):** Cholera, Typhoid, Hepatitis A/E — ganda pani.
- **Vayu-janit (Air-borne):** TB, Corona, Flu.

## 10.3 Nutrition, Sanitation, Key Indicators
- **BMI = Vajan (kg) / (Unchai m)²**; Normal 18.5–24.9; <18.5 underweight; ≥30 obese.
- **Kuposhan:** Stunting (unchai kam), Wasting (vajan kam), Anaemia (khoon ki kami — iron ki kami).
- **IMR (Infant Mortality Rate)** = 1,000 jeevit janm par 1 varsh se kam mrityu; **MMR** = 1,00,000 jeevit janm par matri mrityu; **TFR** = kul prajanan dar (~2.0 replacement level).
- **ASHA:** Accredited Social Health Activist — gaon ki swasthya sahayika; **ANM** — Auxiliary Nurse Midwife; **PHC** — Primary Health Centre (gaun star); **CHC** — Community Health Centre.
- **Teekakaran (Immunisation) schedule:** BCG (janm par, TB), OPV (polio), DPT, Measles (khasra), Hepatitis B.
- **First Aid:** Jalna — thande pani se; Saap katna — shant rakho, turant hospital; **CPR** — hriday/lungs band hone par.
- **Rajasthan — swasthya:** **Mukhyamantri Nishulk Dava Yojana** (muft dava), **Nishulk Jaanch Yojana** (muft test); **108 ambulance** — emergency; **104** — swasthya salah.

**Public Health high-priority:** PM-JAY ₹5 lakh, ASHA, JSY, Poshan Abhiyaan 2018, BMI formula, IMR/MMR paribhasha, Malaria–Anopheles, Polio-mukt 2014.
