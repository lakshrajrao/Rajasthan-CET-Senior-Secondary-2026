> **How to use this diagram:** Sthal ka naam bolo, phir uski pehchan (ek shabd me) bolo — "Kalibangan = jute hue khet", "Ahar = tamba", "Bairat = Ashok". Phir ulta: pehchan suno, sthal batao.

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — nadi kinare base shahar

Rajasthan ka itihaas kile aur rajyon se nahi, **nadi kinare base chhote shaharon** se shuru hota hai. Sabse pehle **Kalibangan** — Ghaggar nadi ke kinare, Hanumangarh me. Yahan ke logon ne khet jotna shuru kiya; unke jute hue khet ke sakshya duniya me iske pehle praman mane jate hain. Unke ghar ke paas agni-vedi (fire altars) mile — matlab dharm aur kheti, dono ek saath.

Uske baad **Ahar** (Udaipur) — yahan logon ne **tamba** (copper) pighlana aur uske upkaran banana seekha; isiliye is sanskriti ko Tamra-Yug kaha gaya aur sthal ko Tamravati. Aas-paas **Gilund** me mudra (seal) mile, aur **Bagore** (Bhilwara) me Bharat ki sabse prachin krishi ke sakshya — matlab Rajasthan me kheti ka itihaas hazaron saal purana hai.

Phir aaya **Bairat (Viratnagar)** — Mahabharat kaal se joda jane wala sthan, jahan se samrat **Ashok ke shilalekh** (Bhabru shilalekh) mile aur ek Bauddh stupa bhi. Yahi Bairat **Matsya Janapada** ki rajdhani tha — Bharat ke 16 Mahajanapadon me se ek.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — sabhyataen nadi kinare kyun bani?

- **Why nadi kinare?** Kheti ke liye pani, peene ke liye pani, aur aane-jane ke liye rasta — teeno ek jagah. Ghaggar Kalibangan ko, Ahar nadi Ahar ko pani deti thi. Isiliye prachin basti nadi kinare hi bani.
- **Why Kalibangan me jute hue khet mile?** Kyunki wahan ke log kheti karte the — aur khet jotne ke nishan mitti me dab gaye, isliye aaj tak surakshit hain. Yah Sindhu Ghati sabhyata ke kisan-jeevan ka seedha praman hai.
- **Why Ahar me tamba?** Ahar ke aas-paas tambe ki khanein thin. Jahan dhatu milti hai, wahan uske upkaran bante hain — isiliye Ahar Tamra-Yug sanskriti bana.
- **Why Bairat me Ashok ke shilalekh?** Ashok apne sandesh (dhamma) pathar par likhwakar pure samrajya me lagwata tha. Bairat us samay ek mahatvapurna sthan tha, isiliye wahan Bhabru shilalekh mila.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — kisi sthal ke sawal ko 3 steps me hal karo

1. **Sthal ka naam dekho** — Kalibangan, Ahar, Bairat, Bagore, Gilund.
2. **Uski ek pehchan jodo** — Kalibangan = jute hue khet; Ahar = tamba; Bairat = Ashok; Bagore = prachin krishi; Gilund = mudra.
3. **Sawal check karo** — sawal "kis visheshta ke liye prasiddh" poochhta hai to pehchan likho; "kahan hai" poochhta hai to jila/nadi likho.

### E. VISUAL MEMORY CONNECTION

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — har rajya ka ek sansthapak

Rajasthan ke har bade rajya ke peeche ek sansthapak ka naam hai. Sabse purana hai **Mewar** — **Guhil / Bappa Rawal** ne 8th century me iski neev rakhi, rajdhani Chittorgarh. Phir aaye **Rawal Jaisal (1156)** — unhone ret me **Jaisalmer** aur uska **Sonar Kila** (sunehra kila) banaya.

**Rao Jodha** ne **1459** me **Jodhpur** basaya (Marwar), aur unke hi vanshaj **Rao Bika** ne **1488** me **Bikaner** basaya. **Rao Madho Singh** ne Kota ko Bundi se alag karke apna rajya banaya. **Dulha Rai** ne Amber ko Kachhwaha rajdhani banaya, aur **Rao Deva** ne Bundi (Hada Chauhan) sthapit kiya. Sabse baad me **Sawai Jai Singh II** ne **1727** me **Jaipur** basaya — usse pehle Amber hi rajdhani thi.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — naye shahar kyun basaye gaye?

- **Why naye shahar?** Har shasak apni rajdhani aise jagah banana chahta tha jo surakshit ho (pahad/kila), pani aur vyapar ke paas ho, aur uske naam se judi ho. Jodhpur, Bikaner, Jaipur — teeno niyojit (planned) shahar isi soch se bane.
- **Why Jaipur 1727 me, Amber ke baad?** Amber pahadon me ghira chhota sthan tha; badhti aabadi aur vyapar ke liye maidan me naya shahar chahiye tha. Isiliye Jai Singh II ne Jaipur basaya.
- **Why Jaisalmer ret me?** Ret ke beech vyapar-marg par sthit hone se Jaisalmer vyapar aur suraksha dono ka kendra bana — Sonar Kila usi ka prateek hai.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — sansthapak-varsh ke sawal ko 3 steps me hal karo

1. **Shahar ka naam dekho** — Jodhpur, Bikaner, Jaipur, Jaisalmer.
2. **Sansthapak jodo** — Jodhpur = Rao Jodha; Bikaner = Rao Bika; Jaipur = Jai Singh II; Jaisalmer = Rawal Jaisal.
3. **Varsh jodo** — 1459 (Jodhpur), 1488 (Bikaner), 1727 (Jaipur), 1156 (Jaisalmer). Naam aur varsh ka joda hi exam poochhta hai.

## Chapter 3 — Yuddh aur Shasak: Khanwa, Haldighati, Dewair, Tarain

   │ VIJAYI      │ VIJAYI      │ Babur       │ (Akbar ka   │ Akbar ki    │ VIJAYI

> **How to use this timeline:** Varsh chhupao, yuddh ka naam bolo; phir naam chhupao, varsh bolo. Har yuddh ke saath dono paksh bolo — yahi exam poochhta hai.

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — teen sau saal ke sangharsh

Rajasthan ke Rajput shasakon ko baar-baar bahar se aaye aakramankariyon se ladna pada. Pehle **Prithviraj Chauhan** — 1191 me pehli Tarain ladai me unhone Muhammad Ghori ko haraya, lekin **1192** me dusri ladai me Ghori vijayi hua. Yah Delhi-Ajmer ke Chauhan rajya ka ant tha.

Phir **Rana Sanga** — 1527 me **Khanwa** ke maidan me Babur se lade aur haare; Rajput shakti ko bada jhatka laga. Unke baad **Maharana Pratap** — 18 June 1576 ko **Haldighati** me Akbar ki sena (Man Singh ke netritva me) se lade. Pratap ne haar nahi mani — sangharsh jari rakha — aur **1582** me **Dewair** me vijay payi. James Tod ne Dewair ko **"Mewar ka Marathon"** kaha.

In yuddhon ke beech **Chittorgarh** do baar ghera gaya. **1568** me Akbar ke aakraman ke samay wahan jauhar hua, Rani Phool Kanwar ke netritva me. In sab se pehle, **Rana Kumbha** (1433–1468) ne Malwa par vijay ke smaran me **1448** me Chittorgarh me **Vijay Stambh** banwaya, aur **Kumbhalgarh** kila banaya — jahan 9 May 1540 ko Maharana Pratap ka janm hua.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — Rajput baar-baar kyun lade?

- **Why Khanwa (1527)?** Babur ne Panipat (1526) ke baad Delhi par kabza kar liya tha; Rana Sanga Rajputana ki sabse badi shakti the. Dono ke beech Bharat ki satta ka sawal tha — isiliye Khanwa hua.
- **Why Haldighati (1576)?** Akbar pure Bharat ko ek samrajya banana chahta tha; Pratap ne Mewar ki swatantrata ke liye samarpan se inkar kar diya. Akbar ne Man Singh ke netritva me sena bheji.
- **Why Dewair (1582) Pratap ki vijay?** Haldighati ke baad Pratap ne pahadi yuddh (guerrilla) ki rananiti apnayi — chhote hamle, peeche hatna, phir hamla. Dewair me yahi rananiti kaam aayi.
- **Why jauhar?** Jab kila girna tay tha, to Rajput auratein dushman ke haath na aane ke liye agni me praan deti thin — yah unke samman ki parampara thi, haar ki nahi.

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — 19 kingdoms become one state

Imagine Rajputana in August 1947: not one Rajasthan, but **19 separate princely states** — Jaipur, Jodhpur, Udaipur, Bikaner, Jaisalmer, Kota, Bundi, Alwar, Bharatpur and more — each with its own ruler, army, treasury and laws, plus the British-ruled Ajmer-Merwara in the middle. Independence meant every ruler had to decide: join India, stay independent, or join Pakistan. Most were too small to survive alone — no money for an army, no administration for a modern state.

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (with V. P. Menon of the States Department) had one job: persuade these rulers to merge, step by step, into bigger unions. The rulers did not all agree at once, so the merger happened in **seven stages over eight years**:

1. **The smallest, most troubled states went first.** Alwar, Bharatpur, Dholpur and Karauli — the eastern states — were shaken by communal disturbance after Partition. The Government of India took over their supervision, and on **17 March 1948** they formed the **Matsya Union** (Matsya = fish; the region was the old Matsya janapada).
2. **Eight days later, the south-eastern states followed** — nine states plus the Kushalgarh chiefship formed the **Rajasthan Union** on **25 March 1948**, with Kota as capital.
3. **Then Mewar (Udaipur) joined** on **18 April 1948**, and the union became the **United State of Rajasthan**. Mewar was among the most senior and prestigious Rajput states, so its joining gave the union legitimacy.
4. **A year later the four big states joined** — Jaipur, Jodhpur, Bikaner, Jaisalmer — on **30 March 1949**, forming **Greater Rajasthan**. Sardar Patel himself inaugurated it. This is why **30 March is Rajasthan Diwas**.
5. **Six weeks later the Matsya Union merged in** (15 May 1949) — now the **United State of Greater Rajasthan**.
6. **Sirohi joined on 26 January 1950** (except Abu and Delwara, which stayed out for the time being).
7. **Finally, on 1 November 1956**, under the States Reorganisation Act, Ajmer-Merwara, Abu-Delwara and the Sunel Tappa (from Madhya Bharat) were merged in — and Sironj went to Madhya Pradesh. The Rajpramukh's office was abolished. **Modern Rajasthan was complete.**

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — why integration took 8 years, not 8 days

- **Why merge at all?** A princely state of a few lakh people could not pay for defence, a police force, courts, schools and roads on its own. Joining a bigger union gave it a shared treasury and administration — and gave India a single, governable Rajasthan instead of 19 fragments on a sensitive border (Rajasthan shares ~1,070 km with Pakistan).
- **Why in stages?** The rulers were not equally willing. The small eastern states were in crisis and merged first; the big, proud states (Jaipur, Jodhpur, Bikaner, Jaisalmer, Udaipur) needed negotiation, guarantees about privy purses and honours, and time. Mewar's Maharana Bhupal Singh first proposed an even bigger union (Rajasthan + Malwa + Saurashtra) before agreeing to join.
- **Why did Ajmer-Merwara wait till 1956?** It was not a princely state — it was directly British-ruled (a Part C state after 1950). It could only be merged by an Act of Parliament, which came with the States Reorganisation Act, 1956, on the recommendation of the States Reorganisation Commission (1953).
- **Why is 30 March the state day, not 1 November?** Because 30 March 1949 is when the four big states joined and "Greater Rajasthan" — recognisably the Rajasthan we know — came into being. 1 November 1956 is the *final* shape, not the birth.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — the merger, step by step

1. **Instrument of Accession / merger agreement:** each ruler signed an agreement to merge his state into the union.
2. **A Rajpramukh was appointed** (usually the senior ruler) as the constitutional head of the union, with an Up-Rajpramukh and a council of ministers.
3. **A capital was fixed** (Alwar → Kota → Udaipur → Jaipur) as the union grew.
4. **The next group of states signed in**, the union was renamed, and the Rajpramukh/ministry were reshuffled.
5. **After 1950** the unions became a Part B state of India; after 1956 the Rajpramukh system was abolished and a Governor replaced him.
