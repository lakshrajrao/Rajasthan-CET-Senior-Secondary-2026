# 1. HISTORY OF RAJASTHAN (राजस्थान का इतिहास) — HIGH PRIORITY

## 1.1 Introduction
Rajasthan ka itihaas — Indus Valley (सिंधु घाटी) se lekar Rajputana, Mughal kaal, Maratha/British kaal aur **30 March 1949** ko Rajasthan ke gathan tak. Exam me sabse zyada questions: **Prachiin kaal (Kalibangan, Ahar, Bairat), Rajput rajya, Maharana Pratap, 1857, praja-mandal aur ekikaran (integration)** se aate hain.

## 1.2 Prachiin Kaal (Ancient Period)
| Sthal / Topic | Mahatvapurna Tathya |
|---|---|
| **Kalibangan** (Hanumangarh) | Indus Valley Civilisation ka sthal; **jute hue khet (ploughed field)** ke sakshya — duniya me pehla; agni-vedi (fire altars); Ghaggar nadi ke kinare |
| **Ahar / Tamravati** (Udaipur) | Tamra-Yug (Chalcolithic) sanskriti; **tambe (copper)** ke upkaran; Ahar nadi kinare |
| **Bairat / Viratnagar** (Jaipur) | Mahabharat kaal se joda jata hai; Ashok ke shilalekh (Bhabru shilalekh); Bauddh stupa |
| **Bagore** (Bhilwara) | Mesolithic (मध्य पाषाण) — Bharat ki sabse prachin krishi ke sakshya |
| **Gilund** (Rajsamand) | Chalcolithic; mudra (seal) ke sakshya |
| **Matsya Janapada** | Bairat rajdhani; 16 Mahajanapadon me se ek |
| **Shakambhari / Sapadalaksha** | Chauhanon ka prachin kshetra |

**Confusion — Kalibangan vs Ahar:** Kalibangan = Indus Valley + ploughed field; Ahar = Copper Age + copper tools. Dono ko mix mat karo.

## 1.6 Ekikaran (Integration of Rajasthan) — EXAM FAVOURITE
| Charan | Tithi | Tathya |
|---|---|---|
| Pehla charan — Matsya Sangh | **17 Mar 1948** | Alwar, Bharatpur, Dholpur, Karauli |
| Dusra charan | 25 Mar 1948 | Kota, Bundi, Jhalawar, Tonk, etc. |
| Teesra charan | 18 Apr 1948 | Udaipur shamil |
| Chautha charan | **30 Mar 1949** | **Brihat Rajasthan** — Jaipur, Jodhpur, Bikaner, Jaisalmer shamil; **Rajasthan Diwas = 30 March** |
| Panchwa charan | 15 May 1949 | Matsya Sangh ka vilay |
| Chhatha charan | 26 Jan 1950 | Sirohi (Abu-Dilwara ko chhodkar) |
| Saatva charan | **1 Nov 1956** | Abu-Dilwara, Ajmer-Merwara shamil; Madhya Pradesh ko Sironj diya gaya — **vartaman Rajasthan ka swaroop** |

**Confused facts:** Rajasthan Diwas 30 March 1949 (Brihat Rajasthan) — Sthapana Diwas ke roop me manaya jata hai. Purna ekikaran 1 Nov 1956 ko hua.

## 1.7 Praja Mandal Andolan
- **1920s–1940s:** Rajyon me loktantrik adhikaron ke liye andolan; **Rajputana ke desi riyasaton** me.
- Pramukh: Jaipur Praja Mandal (1931, Jamnalal Bajaj), Mewar Praja Mandal (1938, Manikya Lal Verma), Marwar Lok Parishad, Kota Praja Mandal.
- **1938 Haripura Congress** ke baad andolan tez hua.

**High-priority revision:** Kalibangan (ploughed field), Haldighati 1576, Khanwa 1527, 30 March 1949, 1857 Nasirabad 28 May, Bijolia–Pathik, Kumbha–Vijay Stambh, Jai Singh II–Jaipur 1727.
