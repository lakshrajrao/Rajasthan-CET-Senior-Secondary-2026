## 3.3 Climate, Soils, Agriculture
- **Mausam:** Mansoon (South-West, June–Sep) — Bharat ki varsha ka ~75%; **Mawsynram/Cherrapunji (Meghalaya)** — sabse zyada varsha.
- **Mitti:** Alluvial (jalodh) — Ganga maidan, sabse upjau; Black (Regur) — kapas ke liye (Deccan); Red; Laterite; Desert.
- **Hari Kranti (Green Revolution):** 1960s; **M.S. Swaminathan** — janak; gehu-chawal; Punjab-Haryana-UP.
- **Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (2016)**, **PM-KISAN (2019)** — ₹6,000/varsh.

## 3.4 Minerals, Industries, Transport
- **Koyla:** Jharia (Jharkhand) — sabse bada koyla kshetra; **Korba, Talcher**.
- **Loha (Iron ore):** Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Karnataka (Bailadila, Kudremukh).
- **Bauxite:** Odisha (Koraput); **Mica:** Jharkhand/Bihar; **Petroleum:** Bombay High, Digboi (Assam — sabse purani refinery).
- **Railway:** 16 zones (2026 tak); pehli rail 1853 Mumbai–Thane.
- **National Highways:** NH-44 sabse lamba (Srinagar–Kanyakumari, ~3,745 km).
- **Ports:** Kandla (Deendayal), Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Visakhapatnam.
