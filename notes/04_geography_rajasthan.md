## 4.2 Physical Divisions (4 bhaugolik khand)
1. **Paschimi Marusthal (Thar)** — ~60% kshetra; Jaisalmer, Barmer, Bikaner, Jodhpur; **Thar — duniya ka sabse ghana aabadi wala marusthal**.
2. **Aravalli Parvat Shrinkhala** — duniya ki **sabse purani parvat shrinkhalaon** me se ek; NE–SW disha; **Guru Shikhar (1,722 m, Mount Abu, Sirohi)** — Rajasthan ki sabse unchi choti.
3. **Purvi Maidan** — Banas, Chambal, Banas-Banganga basin; upjau.
4. **Dakshin-Purvi Pathari Kshetra (Hadoti)** — Kota, Bundi, Jhalawar, Baran; Malwa pathar.

## 4.4 Climate, Soil, Vegetation
- **Jalvaayu:** Shushk (arid) paschim, ardh-shushk madhya, up-aardr purab; varsha paschim (Barmer ~10 cm) se purab (Jhalawar ~100 cm) badhti hai.
- **Mitti:** Marusthaliya (desert), Lal-peeli, Kali (Hadoti — kapas), Jalodh.
- **Van:** Van kshetra ~9.6% (ISFR); **Khejri — rajya vriksh**; **Rohida — rajya pushp**; **Godawan (Great Indian Bustard) — rajya pakshi**; **Chinkara — rajya pashu**; **Unt (Camel) — rajya pashu (ship of desert)** — note: Rajasthan ne 2014 me **unt ko rajya pashu** ghoshit kiya; Chinkara bhi rajya pashu ke roop me ullekhit hota hai — exam me dono me se ek option hota hai, context dekho.
- **Rashtriya Udyan / Abhayaranya:** Ranthambore NP (Sawai Madhopur), Sariska Tiger Reserve (Alwar), Keoladeo NP (Bharatpur — UNESCO), Desert NP (Jaisalmer), Mukundra Hills NP (Kota).

## 4.5 Minerals & Economy-Geography
- **Khanij:** Rajasthan khanij sampada me agrani — **Zinc, Lead (Zawar, Udaipur — Bharat me sabse bada)**, Marble (Makrana, Rajsamand), Sandstone, Limestone, Gypsum, Rock Phosphate, **Jaisalmer me natural gas / petroleum (Barmer — Mangala oil field)**.
- **Urja:** **Solar — Bharat me No.1** (Nov 2026: kul renewable ~51.6 GW, solar ~45 GW — RE-SAF award Nov 2026, source: ET Rajasthan 6 Nov 2026); **Bhadla Solar Park (Jodhpur)** — duniya ke sabse bade solar parkon me se ek.
- **Sinchai:** **Indira Gandhi Nahar (IGNP)** — Rajasthan ki sabse badi nahar pariyojana (Harike Barrage, Punjab se); **Chambal pariyojana**; **Bisalpur bandh** (Tonk — Jaipur ko pani).
- **Fasal:** Bajra (sabse zyada utpadan — Bharat me No.1), Jowar, Gehun, Sarso (Rajasthan **sarso me No.1**), Jeera, Dhaniya, Isabgol.

**High-priority revision:** Guru Shikhar 1,722 m, Sambhar = sabse badi khari jheel, Banas = keval Rajasthan me sabse lambi, Thar 60%, Khejri/Rohida/Godawan, Bhadla Solar, IGNP, Bajra/Sarso No.1.
