## 7.1 Physics (भौतिकी) — SI Units, Motion, Light, Sound, Electricity
| Rashi (Quantity) | SI Unit | Symbol / Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Lambaai (Length) | metre | m |
| Dravyaman (Mass) | kilogram | kg |
| Samay (Time) | second | s |
| Tapman (Temperature) | kelvin | K |
| Vidyut dhara (Current) | ampere | A |
| Prakash tivrata | candela | cd |
| Bal (Force) | newton | N; **F = m × a** |
| Karya / Urja (Work/Energy) | joule | J; **W = F × s**; KE = ½mv²; PE = mgh |
| Shakti (Power) | watt | W; **P = W/t**; 1 HP = 746 W |
| Dabav (Pressure) | pascal | Pa; P = F/A |
| Aavritti (Frequency) | hertz | Hz |
| Vidyut vibhav (Voltage) | volt | V; **V = IR (Ohm's Law)** |
| Pratirodh (Resistance) | ohm | Ω; R = ρL/A |
| Vidyut shakti | watt | **P = VI = I²R** |
| Aavesh (Charge) | coulomb | C |

**Gati (Motion):** v = u + at; s = ut + ½at²; v² = u² + 2as. **Gurutvakarshan:** g = 9.8 m/s². **Newton ke 3 niyam:** 1) Jadta (inertia) 2) F=ma 3) Kriya–Pratikriya.
**Prakash (Light):** Prakash ki gati (vacuum) **3×10⁸ m/s**. **Prativartan (Reflection)** — angle of incidence = angle of reflection. **Apvartan (Refraction)** — Snell's law; mirage, rainbow. **Lens:** Uttal (convex — abhisari/converging), Avtal (concave — apsari/diverging). **Manav netra:** Retina par ulta pratibimb; **Myopia (nikat drishti)** — concave lens; **Hypermetropia (door drishti)** — convex lens. **Varn (Colour):** Primary — Red, Green, Blue; Surya prakash ke 7 rang — VIBGYOR; Akash neela — **prakash ka prakirnan (scattering)**.
**Dhvani (Sound):** Dhvani ki gati hawa me **~343 m/s** (20°C); vacuum me nahi chalti; **Ultrasonic > 20,000 Hz**; **SONAR** — ultrasonic; **Echo** — pratidhvani; Frequency badhe to pitch badhe.
**Vidyut (Electricity):** Shreni (series) me current samaan, vibhav batata hai; Samantar (parallel) me vibhav samaan. **Fuse** — suraksha; **Earth wire** — hari. **AC vs DC:** Bharat me ghar ki bijli **220 V, 50 Hz AC**. **Transformer** — voltage badalne wala (step-up/step-down), AC par hi kaam karta hai. **Bulb:** Tungsten filament; CFL/LED — urja bachat.
**Chumbak (Magnet):** Loha, nickel, cobalt — chumbakiya; Prithvi ek bada chumbak hai; **Compass** — uttar-dakshin.
**Dabav aur Utplavan:** **Archimedes ka siddhant** — utplavan bal = visthapit drav ka bhar; **Pascal ka niyam** — hydraulic lift/brake.

**Confused:** Myopia = concave; Hypermetropia = convex. Series me R = R1+R2; Parallel me 1/R = 1/R1+1/R2. Sound vacuum me nahi, light chalti hai.
