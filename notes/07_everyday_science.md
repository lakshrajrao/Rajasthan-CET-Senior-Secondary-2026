# Chapter 1 — SI Units, Motion, Force & Energy

**Official topic:** physics — SI units, motion, force, work, energy, power. **Bank MCQs:** `07_EVERYDAY_SCIENCE-001, 07_EVERYDAY_SCIENCE-008, 07_EVERYDAY_SCIENCE-010, 07_EVERYDAY_SCIENCE-012` (generated).

## A. VISUAL INTRODUCTION — the measuring toolkit

```
Har maap ke liye ek auzar, har auzar ki ek ikai (SI unit):

   LAMBAI ──► scale (metre, m)        SAMAY ──► stopwatch (second, s)
   VAZAN  ──► tarazu (kilogram, kg)    TAPMAN ──► thermometer (kelvin, K)
   BAL    ──► spring balance (newton, N)   BIJLI ──► ammeter (ampere, A)

   Sutra = ikaiyon ka khel:  F = m × a  →  N = kg × m/s²
                            W = F × s  →  J = N × m
                            P = W / t  →  W (watt) = J / s
```

> **How to use this diagram:** Cover the units and say, for each quantity, its SI unit and symbol. Then cover the quantities and say each unit from its symbol. That is retrieval practice, not reading.

## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — ek bus, ek thela, ek bijli ka bill

Subah ek bus Jaipur se Ajmer ja rahi hai — 135 km, 3 ghante me. Uski gati = doori ÷ samay = 45 km/h. Raste me ek aadmi ek bhara hua thela dhakel raha hai — thela bhari hai, isliye use zyada **bal (force)** lagana pad raha hai. Wahi thela khali hota to kam bal lagta. Yahi Newton ka dusra niyam hai: **bal = dravyaman × twaran (F = m × a)** — jitna bhari, utna zyada bal. Shaam ko ghar me bijli ka bill aata hai — bill **shakti (power)** aur samay par banta hai: ek 100 watt ka bulb 10 ghante jale to 1 unit bijli kharch hoti hai. Gati, bal, karya, shakti — ye sab ek hi kahani ke hisse hain: **kuchh hila, to bal laga; bal ne doori tay ki, to karya hua; karya jaldi hua, to shakti zyada.**

## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — sutra aise kyun hain?

- **SI units ek jaise kyun?** Kyunki vigyan me har desh ek hi maap bole — metre har jagah metre hai. Isse galti nahi hoti: 1 kg Jaipur me bhi 1 kg hai, London me bhi.
- **F = ma kyun?** Bal wahi hai jo dravyaman ko twaran deta hai. Bina bal ke koi vastu apni gati nahi badalti (Newton ka pehla niyam — jadta/inertia). Bhari vastu ko utna hi twaran dene ke liye zyada bal chahiye — isiliye bhara thela dhakelna mushkil hai.
- **Gatishil urja (KE) me v² kyun?** Kyunki gati doguni ho to urja chauguni hoti hai — isiliye 100 km/h ki takkar 50 km/h se **chaar guna** zyada khatarnak hoti hai, doguni nahi. Sutra KE = ½mv² yahi batata hai.
- **1 HP = 746 W kyun?** Horsepower James Watt ki paribhasha hai — ek ghode ki shakti ke barabar. Aaj bhi motor, pump aur engine ki shakti HP me likhi jati hai.

## D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — koi bhi motion/formula sawal 4 steps me

1. **Diya hua likho** — kya diya hai (v, t, m, a) aur kya nikalna hai.
2. **Sutra chuno** — doori ke liye s = v × t; bal ke liye F = m × a; karya ke liye W = F × s.
3. **Ikai dekho** — km/h ko m/s me badlo jab zarurat ho (km/h × 5/18 = m/s); gram ko kg me.
4. **Ganna karo aur ikai likho** — bina ikai ke uttar adhura hai (10 N, 100 m, 746 W).

**Worked example (bank 012):** Vastu 20 m/s se chal rahi hai; 5 sec me doori = v × t = 20 × 5 = **100 m**.
**Worked example:** 5 kg ki vastu ko 2 m/s² twaran dene ke liye bal = 5 × 2 = **10 N**.
**Worked example:** 2 kg, 3 m/s ki gati se — KE = ½ × 2 × 3² = **9 J**.

## E. VISUAL MEMORY CONNECTION

Soch lo: **bal = dhakka, karya = dhakke se chalna, shakti = kitni jaldi chala.** Ek hi thela — dhakka (F), thela aage badha (W = F × s), aur jaldi badha to shakti (P = W/t) zyada. Teeno ek hi kahani hain, alag-alag hisse nahi.

# Chapter 2 — Light: Reflection, Refraction, Lenses & the Eye

**Official topic:** reflection of light, laws, dispersion, lenses, vision defects. **Bank MCQs:** `07_EVERYDAY_SCIENCE-003, 07_EVERYDAY_SCIENCE-004, 07_EVERYDAY_SCIENCE-005` (generated).

## A. VISUAL INTRODUCTION — ek kiran ka safar

```
Suraj ki roshni → PRISM → 7 rang (VIBGYOR)
   Violet · Indigo · Blue · Green · Yellow · Orange · Red

MIRROR (prativartan):  aane wali kiran  ↘  |  jaane wali kiran  ↗
                       angle of incidence = angle of reflection

LENS:  Uttal (convex, beech me mota) ──► roshni ko EK jagah milata hai (abhisari)
       Avtal (concave, beech me patla) ──► roshni ko FAILATA hai (apsari)

EYE:  roshni → cornea → lens → RETINA par ulta pratibimb → dimaag seedha karta hai
```

> **How to use this diagram:** Cover the labels and say, for each lens shape, whether it converges or diverges, and which eye defect it corrects. Then redraw the prism rainbow from memory. That is retrieval practice, not reading.

## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — aaina, chashma aur indradhanush

Subah tum aaine me dekhte ho — roshni tumhare chehre se aaine par padti hai aur wapas aati hai, isiliye tum dikhte ho. Yahi **prativartan (reflection)** hai: aane ka kon = jaane ka kon. Barish ke baad aakash me indradhanush dikhta hai — suraj ki safed roshni paani ki boondon se guzarti hai aur **7 rangon me bikhar jati hai (dispersion)**. Wahi 7 rang prism se bhi dikhte hain. Ab chashme ki baat: jo baccha paas ka saaf dekhta hai par door ka dhundhla, use **Myopia (nikat drishti)** hai — uski aankh ki roshni retina se **pehle** mil jati hai; **avtal (concave)** lens use peeche dhakel kar retina par le aata hai. Jo door ka saaf dekhta hai par paas ka nahi, use **Hypermetropia (door drishti)** hai — roshni retina ke **peeche** milti hai; **uttal (convex)** lens use aage khinch kar retina par le aata hai.

## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — roshni aise kyun karti hai?

- **Aakash neela kyun dikhta hai?** Suraj ki roshni me neela rang sabse zyada **prakirn (scatter)** hota hai — chhoti tarang-dairdhya (wavelength) wale rang zyada bikhate hain, aur neela sabse chhota hai. Hawa ke kan roshni ko chaaron ore bikher dete hain; hamari aankh tak neela zyada pahunchta hai, isliye aakash neela dikhta hai. (NCERT Class 10, Human Eye and the Colourful World.)
- **Prism se 7 rang kyun?** Safed roshni ek rang nahi, 7 rangon ka mishran hai. Prism me har rang alag kon se mudta hai (apvartan/refraction) — baingani sabse zyada, lal sabse kam — isliye rang alag-alag dikhte hain.
- **Myopia me concave lens kyun?** Myopia me aankh ki goli lambi hoti hai ya lens zyada shaktishali, isliye roshni retina se pehle mil jati hai. Concave lens roshni ko failata hai, to milne ki jagah peeche khisak kar retina par aa jati hai.
- **Retina par ulta pratibimb kyun, phir bhi seedha kyun dikhta hai?** Aankh ka lens uttal hai — uttal lens ulta pratibimb banata hai. Dimaag is ulte pratibimb ko seedha samajhta hai; ye dimaag ka kaam hai, aankh ka nahi.

## D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — lens aur drishti-dosh pehchano, 3 steps me

1. **Samasya dekho** — paas ka dhundhla = Myopia; door ka dhundhla = Hypermetropia.
2. **Roshni kahan milti hai?** — retina se pehle = Myopia; retina ke peeche = Hypermetropia.
3. **Lens chuno** — pehle milti hai to **failao (concave)**; peeche milti hai to **milao (convex)**.

## E. VISUAL MEMORY CONNECTION

Prism ko **chhata (umbrella)** samjho: suraj ki ek safed kiran chhate me ghusti hai aur 7 rangon wali chhata khul jati hai — VIBGYOR. Aur lens ko **chashme ke sheeshe** samjho: mota beech wala (convex) roshni ko ek jagah milata hai, patla beech wala (concave) use failata hai.

# Chapter 3 — Sound

**Official topic:** physics — sound, its speed, ultrasonic, SONAR, echo. **Bank MCQs:** `07_EVERYDAY_SCIENCE-006` (generated).

## A. VISUAL INTRODUCTION — dhvani ek lehar hai

```
Awaaz ka safar (hawa me):

   Speaker ──► )))))) ──► hawa ke kan ──► )))))) ──► kaan
              dabav (compression)    virav (rarefaction) — aage-peeche, aage-peeche

   VACUUM me:  Speaker ──► (kuchh nahi) ──► kaan  ✗  — dhvani nahi chalti!
   Bijli (light) vacuum me chalti hai; dhvani nahi. Yahi antar hai.
```

> **How to use this diagram:** Cover the labels and say why sound cannot travel in a vacuum, and what carries it in air. Then redraw the wave from memory.

## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — bijli pehle, garaj baad me

Baarish me bijli chamakti hai aur garaj **baad me** sunai deti hai — kyunki prakash (3 × 10⁸ m/s) dhvani (343 m/s) se lakhon guna tez hai. Pahadon me chillao to awaaz wapas aati hai — **echo (pratidhvani)**. Samudra me jahaz SONAR se machhliyon aur doobe hue jahaz ka pata lagate hain — **ultrasonic** tarangein bhej kar, unke wapas aane ka samay naap kar. Kutte 20,000 Hz se upar ki awaaz sun lete hain jo hume sunai nahi deti — wahi **ultrasonic** hai.

## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — dhvani aise kyun karti hai?

- **Dhvani ko madhyam kyun chahiye?** Dhvani hawa ke kanon ke aage-peeche hilne se chalti hai — kan nahi, to lehar nahi. Vacuum me kan hi nahi hote, isliye dhvani nahi chalti. Prakash ko kan nahi chahiye — woh khud ek vidyut-chumbakiya lehar hai.
- **Frequency badhe to pitch badhe kyun?** Pitch = awaaz ki unchai (sur). Zyada frequency matlab kan zyada baar hilte hain — dimaag use "teekhi" awaaz samajhta hai. Mahila ki awaaz purush se teekhi isiliye hoti hai (frequency zyada).
- **Echo kab sunai deta hai?** Jab awaaz kisi badi satah se takra kar wapas aaye aur mool awaaz se alag sunai de. Chhoti doori par echo mool awaaz me mil jata hai — isiliye kamre me echo nahi, pahadon me sunai deta hai.

## D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — echo se doori naapna

1. **Awaaz bhejo, samay naapo** — awaaz jaane aur wapas aane ka kul samay (t) naapo.
2. **Kul doori = gati × samay** — 343 × t.
3. **Aadha karo** — kyunki awaaz jaane aur aane dono ka rasta tay karti hai: **doori = (343 × t) ÷ 2**.

## E. VISUAL MEMORY CONNECTION

Dhvani ko **hawa me tairti lehar** samjho — jaise talab me pathar phenkne par lehrein banti hain. Pathar = awaaz ka srot; lehrein = hawa ke kanon ka hilna. Talab khali ho (vacuum) to lehar banti hi nahi.

## F. IMPORTANT FACTS

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Dhvani ki gati hawa me (20°C) | **~343 m/s** |

## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — ghar ki bijli, fuse aur jahaz

Ghar me bijli aati hai — **220 V, 50 Hz AC**. Tum switch dabate ho, circuit band hota hai, bulb jalta hai. Kabhi zyada load padne par **fuse** pigal jata hai — fuse ek kamzor taar hai jo pehle jal jata hai, taaki ghar ke upkaran bach jayein. Hari taar **earth wire** hai — bijli ko zameen me le jati hai, jhatke se bachati hai. Ab jahaz ki baat: lohe ka bada jahaz paani par tairta hai, jabki lohe ki keel doob jati hai — kyunki jahaz ke andar khokhli jagah hai, uska ausat ghanatva paani se kam ho jata hai. Aur jab tum car ka brake dabate ho, **Pascal ka niyam** kaam karta hai — band drav me dabav har jagah barabar pahunchta hai, isiliye halka sa dabav bhi bade brake ko rok deta hai.

## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — bijli aur dabav ke niyam

- **Series me current samaan kyun?** Kyunki rasta ek hi hai — current ke paas jaane ka doosra rasta nahi. Vibhav (voltage) dono pratirodhon me bat jata hai. Parallel me ulta: rasta do hai, isliye current bat jata hai aur vibhav dono par samaan rehta hai.
- **Fuse kyun pigalta hai?** Fuse ki taar ka galnank (melting point) kam hota hai. Zyada current aane par taar garam hokar pigal jati hai aur circuit toot jata hai — upkaran bach jate hain.
- **Transformer sirf AC par kyun kaam karta hai?** Transformer badalte hue chumbakiya kshetra se kaam karta hai. AC ki disha badalti rehti hai, isliye kshetra badalta hai; DC sthir rehta hai, isliye transformer DC par kaam nahi karta.
- **Loha paani me doobta hai, jahaz kyun nahi?** Koi vastu tairti hai jab uska ausat ghanatva paani se kam ho. Jahaz khokhla hota hai — loha + hawa ka ausat ghanatva paani se kam ho jata hai. Lohe ki keel thos hoti hai — ghanatva zyada, doob jati hai. (Yahi PYQ 2024-P2 Q16 ka siddhant hai: "metals sink — density".)

## D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — pratirodh aur circuit ke sawal 3 steps me

1. **Series ya parallel pehchano** — ek rasta = series; do raste = parallel.
2. **Sutra lagao** — series: R = R1 + R2; parallel: 1/R = 1/R1 + 1/R2.
3. **Ohm's Law se jodo** — V = IR se voltage, current ya resistance nikalo.

**Worked example (bank 011):** Series me 4 Ω aur 6 Ω — kul R = 4 + 6 = **10 Ω**. (Parallel me yahi 2.4 Ω hota — bank ke galat vikalp me yahi diya hai.)
**Worked example:** V = 220 V, R = 100 Ω → I = V/R = **2.2 A**.

## E. VISUAL MEMORY CONNECTION

## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — har cheez parmanu se bani hai

Tumhari kitaab, tumhara shareer, hawa, paani — sab kuchh **parmanu (atom)** se bana hai. Parmanu ke kendra (nucleus) me proton (+) aur neutron (0) hain; electron (−) bahar kakshaon me ghoomte hain. ## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — parmanu sankhya hi pehchan kyun?

- **Proton hi pehchan kyun?** Kyunki proton ki sankhya badle to tatva hi badal jata hai — 6 proton Carbon hai, 7 proton Nitrogen. Neutron badle to sirf samasthanik (isotope) banta hai, tatva nahi badalta. Electron badle to aayan (ion) banta hai, tatva nahi badalta.
- **Hawa me Nitrogen sabse zyada kyun?** Ye prithvi ke itihas ka parinam hai — hawa ka sangathan samay ke saath bana. Iska koi "kaaran" nahi jo sutra se nikle; ye ek maapa hua tathya hai (definition/fact — isiliye ratna padta hai: 78% N₂, 21% O₂).
- **Avogadro number kyun?** Parmanu bahut chhote hote hain — ek chammach paani me arabon parmanu hain. Unhe ginne ke liye ek badi ikai chahiye: **1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ kan** — jaise 1 darjan = 12, waise hi 1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³.

## D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — parmanu sankhya aur dravyaman sankhya nikalo

1. **Z (atomic number) = proton ki sankhya** — ye tatva ki pehchan hai.
2. **A (mass number) = proton + neutron** — A − Z = neutron ki sankhya.
3. **Electron (tatstha parmanu me) = proton** — charge santulan ke liye.

**Worked example:** Carbon ka Z = 6, A = 12 → proton 6, neutron 12 − 6 = **6**, electron **6**.

## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — jang lagna, gas jalna

Baarish me chhoda hua lohe ka gate kuchh dinon me bhura pad jata hai — **jang (rust)** lag gaya. Jang tab lagta hai jab loha, oxygen aur paani teeno milte hain. ## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — jang aur jalan

Lohe ki jang khokhli aur bhurbhuri hoti hai — girti rehti hai, isliye loha andar tak kha jata hai.

- **LPG/CNG jalne par garmi kyun milti hai?** Jalan (combustion) ek ushmakshepi (exothermic) abhikriya hai — hydrocarbon oxygen se jud kar CO₂ + paani + garmi banata hai. Isiliye gas chulha khana pakata hai.

## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — nimbu, sabun aur antacid

Subah nimbu paani peete ho — khatta lagta hai, kyunki nimbu me **citric acid** hai. Nahate samay sabun lagate ho — woh chikna aur kadwa sa lagta hai, kyunki sabun **kshariya (basic)** hota hai. Zyada mirch-masala kha liya to pet me jalan hoti hai — pet me acid badh gaya. Tab **antacid** lete ho — **Milk of Magnesia (Mg(OH)₂)** — jo acid ko neutral kar deta hai. Rasoi me baking soda se cake phoolta hai, aur dhone ka soda kapdon se mail nikalta hai. Ye sab ek hi vigyan hai: **acid aur base ek dusre ko neutral karte hain.**

## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — acid-base aise kyun karte hain?

- **Acid neela litmus lal kyun karta hai?** Acid paani me H⁺ aayan deta hai; litmus ek rang-badlane wala padarth (indicator) hai jo H⁺ ki maujudgi me lal ho jata hai. Base OH⁻ deta hai, to lal litmus neela ho jata hai.
- **Antacid jalan kyun mitata hai?** Pet ka acid (HCl) zyada ho gaya hai. Antacid (Mg(OH)₂, ek base) us acid se jud kar namak + paani banata hai — **neutralisation**. Acid kam, jalan kam.
- **Sirka aur nimbu dono acid, phir alag kyun?** Dono me alag acid hai — sirke me **acetic acid**, nimbu me **citric acid**. Acid ka naam uske srot se aata hai; gun (khatta, litmus badalna) ek jaise hain.

## D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — acid ya base pehchano, 3 steps me

1. **Swad/rang dekho** — khatta = acid; kadwa/chikna = base (swad chakhna exam me nahi, sirf samajhne ke liye).
2. **Litmus test** — neela → lal = acid; lal → neela = base.
3. **pH dekho** — pH < 7 acid; = 7 neutral; > 7 base.

## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — taamba ki taar, peetal ke bartan

Ghar me bijli taamba (Cu) ki taar se aati hai — kyunki taamba bijli ka achha suchalak hai aur khinch kar taar banta hai. Rasoi me peetal ke bartan hain — peetal ek **mishr dhatu (alloy)** hai, taamba + jasta se banta hai; shuddh taambe se zyada sakht aur chamkila hota hai. Lohe ka gate saalon chalta hai, par gandhak (sulphur) ka tukda haath me lete hi toot jata hai — gandhak adhatu hai, bhangur hai. Dhatu aur adhatu ka antar roz dikhta hai — bas pehchanne ki nazar chahiye.

## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — dhatu aise kyun hote hain?

- **Dhatu bijli kyun chalate hain?** Dhatu ke parmanuon ke bahari electron aazad ghoomte hain (free electrons) — voltage milte hi ye electron behne lagte hain, wahi current hai. Adhatu me electron bandhe hote hain, isliye current nahi behta.
- **Dhatu chamkile kyun?** Dhatu ki satah par aazad electron roshni ko paravartit karte hain — isiliye dhatu chamakta hai. Adhatu me aazad electron nahi, isliye chamak nahi.
- **Mishr dhatu shuddh dhatu se sakht kyun?** Alloy me alag aakar ke parmanu mil jate hain — parmanuon ki paratien aasani se khisakti nahi, isliye alloy sakht hota hai. Isiliye bartan aur auzar alloy se bante hain, shuddh dhatu se nahi.
- **Dhatu paani me kyun doobte hain?** Zyadatar dhatuon ka ghanatva paani se zyada hota hai (Ch 4, Archimedes). Isiliye lohe ki keel doobti hai — aur khokhla jahaz tairta hai (ausat ghanatva kam).

## D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — dhatu ya adhatu pehchano, 3 steps me

1. **Chamak dekho** — chamkila = dhatu ki ore ishara.
2. **Bijli/garmi test** — suchalak = dhatu; nahi = adhatu.
3. **Peet kar dekho (soch me)** — patla hota hai = dhatu (malleable); toot jata hai = adhatu (brittle).

## E. VISUAL MEMORY CONNECTION

## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — carbon har jagah hai

- **CNG/LPG jalne par garmi kyun?** Ye hydrocarbon hain — jalne par CO₂ + paani + garmi bante hain (Ch 6, combustion).

## D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — sabun se dhona, 3 steps me

3. **Paani se dho lo** — sabun ka paani-wala sira mail ko paani me kheench kar le jata hai.

## A. VISUAL INTRODUCTION — the lock-and-key picture


## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — a transfusion that must not go wrong

A patient in a hospital in Jaipur has lost blood in an accident and needs a transfusion. The doctor does not simply take any blood bag off the shelf — she first checks the patient's blood group, then the donor bag's group, and then runs a **cross-match** (mixing a drop of each in the lab to see if clumping happens). Why so careful? Because in 1900, Karl Landsteiner discovered that mixing the wrong bloods makes red cells **clump together (agglutination)** — and in a patient's veins, clumped cells block blood vessels, break apart (haemolysis), damage the kidneys and can kill. Before Landsteiner, transfusions were a gamble; after him, they became a matching exercise. Forty years later (1940), Landsteiner and Alexander Wiener found a second matching system — the **Rh factor** — which explained why some transfusions still went wrong even when the ABO groups matched, and why some babies were born anaemic to healthy mothers.

---

## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — why blood clumps, and why groups exist

- **Why does wrong blood clump?** Your immune system treats anything "not self" as an invader. The antigens (A/B) are sugar molecules on the RBC surface; the antibodies (anti-A/anti-B) float in the plasma. If donor RBCs carry antigen A and your plasma carries anti-A, the antibodies bind to the donor cells and glue them together — agglutination — exactly as they would glue together invading bacteria. The clumps block small vessels and the cells rupture (haemolysis), releasing haemoglobin that damages the kidneys.
- **Why does your plasma contain antibodies against antigens you don't have?** These are "natural" antibodies: they appear early in life, probably after exposure to similar sugar molecules on gut bacteria and food. You never need a transfusion to develop them — that is why the *first* wrong transfusion is already dangerous (unlike Rh, where the first exposure usually only sensitises).
- **Why do blood groups exist at all?** The ABO genes code for enzymes that add different sugars to the RBC surface. The variation is inherited (Mendel's laws — see §D) and persists in populations; there is no single "best" group, which is why all four survive.
- **Why is the Rh factor separate?** Rh(D) is a different protein on the RBC surface, inherited independently of ABO. About 95% of Indians are Rh+ (have it). An Rh− person has no anti-Rh antibody naturally — it appears only *after* exposure to Rh+ blood (transfusion, or an Rh+ baby). That is why Rh problems show up in the *second* Rh+ pregnancy, not the first.

---

## D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — transfusion, step by step

1. **Type the patient:** test the patient's RBCs against known anti-A, anti-B and anti-D sera — clumping tells you which antigens are present.
2. **Type the donor bag** the same way.
3. **Match by the rule:** the donor's RBC antigens must not meet an antibody in the patient's plasma. (Donor plasma matters less for red-cell transfusion because the bag is mostly RBCs.)
4. **Cross-match:** mix donor RBCs with patient plasma in the lab; no clumping = safe to transfuse.
5. **Transfuse slowly, watch the patient** — fever, chills or back pain in the first minutes means stop immediately (a reaction is happening).
6. **In pregnancy:** if the mother is Rh− and the baby is Rh+, the mother's immune system can be sensitised at delivery; the *next* Rh+ baby's RBCs are then attacked (haemolytic disease of the newborn / erythroblastosis fetalis). Prevention: anti-D immunoglobulin given to the Rh− mother, which clears the baby's Rh+ cells before she can make antibodies.

**Inheritance (Mendel, one step):** ABO is controlled by one gene with three alleles — Iᴬ, Iᴮ (both dominant) and i (recessive). Genotypes: IᴬIᴬ / Iᴬi = A; IᴮIᴮ / Iᴮi = B; IᴬIᴮ = AB; ii = O. So two group-O parents can only have group-O children; an AB parent can never have an O child.

---

## E. VISUAL MEMORY CONNECTION

Think of antigens as **name-badges** and antibodies as **security guards**. A group-A cell wears an "A" badge; its plasma guards throw out anyone wearing a "B" badge. Group O cells wear **no badge at all** — so no guard objects to them, and they can enter any bloodstream (universal donor). Group AB cells wear **both badges** — but their plasma has **no guards at all**, so they accept everyone (universal recipient). **No badge = goes everywhere; no guards = lets everyone in.**

| Antibody present from birth vs after exposure | ABO antibodies are natural (present without transfusion). Anti-Rh appears **only after exposure** to Rh+ blood. |
| Landsteiner 1900 vs Nobel 1930 | Discovered ABO in **1900–01**; won the Nobel Prize in **1930**. Rh discovered **1940** (with Wiener). |
| Antigen vs antibody | Antigen = on the **RBC surface** (the badge). Antibody = in the **plasma** (the guard). |
| Malaria vector | Anopheles mosquito (not Aedes — Aedes spreads dengue). *(Related bank Q 029 — kept here because students mix the two.)* |
| Bombay blood group (hh) | Very rare; lacks the H antigen, so even group-O blood is incompatible. **Beyond CET syllabus** — noted only so the genotype rules above are not over-generalised. |

## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — ek dil, ek haddi, ek hormone

Tumhara dil ek minute me 72 baar dhadakta hai — bina ruke, bina thake, janm se mrityu tak. Uske 4 kaksh hain: dayein hissa khoon ko fefdon me bhejta hai oxygen lene, bayein hissa oxygen wala khoon pure shareer me bhejta hai. Tumhare shareer me 206 haddiyan hain — bachpan me ~300 hoti hain, bade hote-hote kuchh jud jati hain. Sabse badi haddi **femur** (jangh ki) hai, sabse chhoti **stapes** (kaan ki) — chawal ke dane se bhi chhoti. Dimaag ke 3 hisse hain: cerebrum sochta hai, cerebellum santulan rakhta hai, medulla dil aur saans ko chalata hai. Aur shareer me chhote se granthiyan hormone banati hain — **pituitary (master gland)** sabko chalati hai, **pancreas** insulin banata hai (kami se diabetes), **adrenal** adrenaline banata hai (khatre me "ladna ya bhagna").

## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — shareer aise kyun bana hai?

- **Dil ke 4 kaksh kyun?** Kyunki oxygen wala aur bina-oxygen wala khoon milna nahi chahiye. 4 kaksh dono ko alag rakhte hain — dayein taraf bina-oxygen khoon (fefdon ki ore), bayein taraf oxygen khoon (shareer ki ore). Agar mil jayein to shareer ko kam oxygen milega.
- **Bachche me haddiyan zyada kyun?** Bachche ki haddiyan naram aur alag-alag hoti hain; bade hote-hote kuchh haddiyan jud kar ek ho jati hain — isiliye vayask me 206 reh jati hain.
- **Pituitary "master gland" kyun?** Kyunki ye dusri granthiyon ko hormone bhej kar chalati hai — thyroid, adrenal aur sex glands sab iske niyantran me hain. Isiliye ise master kehte hain.
- **Vitamin ki kami se rog kyun?** Vitamin shareer ko thodi matra me chahiye, par bina unke kaam ruk jate hain — vitamin C bina collagen nahi banta (scurvy), vitamin D bina haddi me calcium nahi jamta (rickets), vitamin A bina aankh ki roshni kamzor hoti hai (ratondhi).

## D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — khoon ka safar aur vitamin ki pehchan

**Khoon ka safar (ek chakkar):**
1. Shareer se bina-oxygen khoon → dil ke **dayein** hisse me.
2. Dayein hissa → **fefde** (oxygen milta hai, CO₂ nikalta hai).
3. Oxygen khoon → dil ke **baye** hisse me.
4. Bayein hissa → pure **shareer** me (oxygen deta hai, CO₂ leta hai) → wapas step 1.

**Vitamin ki kami pehchano:**
1. Rog dekho — ratondhi / beriberi / scurvy / rickets / khoon na jamna / anaemia.
2. Table se milao (§F) — A / B1 / C / D / K / B12.

## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — machhar, teeka aur ek gaon

## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — rog aise kyun failte hain?

- **Opioid receptors kya hain?** (PYQ 2024-P2 Q8) Opioids (afeem jaise padarth) shareer me khaas **receptor** se judte hain jo dimaag aur aant me hote hain — isiliye ye dard aur neend par asar karte hain. (Standard fact, NCERT Class 12, Human Health and Disease.)

## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — ek jungle, ek nadi, ek andolan

Ek jungle me ghaas ugti hai, hiran ghaas khata hai, sher hiran khata hai — ye **khady shrinkhala (food chain)** hai. Sher mar jaye to bacteria/fungi use mitti me mila dete hain — **apghatak (decomposer)** — aur mitti se phir ghaas ugti hai. Ye chakra hai. Ab sheher ki baat: factory ka dhuan (CO, SO₂, NOx, PM2.5) hawa ko, industrial waste paani ko ganda karta hai. **Greenhouse gas (CO₂, CH₄, N₂O)** garmi ko rok kar **global warming** karti hai. Upar **ozone parat (O₃)** suraj ki UV kiranon se bachati hai — **CFC** use chhed karta hai. 1973 me Uttarakhand me mahilaon ne pedon ko gale laga liya — **Chipko Andolan (Sunderlal Bahuguna)** — ped katne se bachane ke liye. Aur 1973 me hi **Project Tiger** shuru hua — bagh bachane ke liye.

## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — paryavaran aise kyun kaam karta hai?

- **Ozone parat UV se kyun bachati hai?** Ozone (O₃) suraj ki parabaingani (UV) kiranon ko sokh leta hai — bina iske UV seedhe zameen par aakar twacha aur paudhon ko nuksan pahunchati. CFC ozone ko tod deta hai — isiliye "ozone chhed" banta hai.
- **Greenhouse gas garmi kyun badhati hai?** CO₂, CH₄ aur N₂O suraj ki garmi ko andar aane dete hain, par bahar jaane se rokte hain — jaise greenhouse (kacch ka ghar) garmi rokta hai. Isiliye dharti garam ho rahi hai (global warming).
- **Food chain chhoti kyun hoti hai?** Kyunki har star par ~90% urja khatm ho jati hai (sirf ~10% aage jata hai — 10% ka niyam, NCERT Class 10, standard reference). Isiliye chain me 4–5 se zyada star nahi hote.
- **Pyramid of numbers ped par ulta kyun?** Aam taur par neeche zyada jeev, upar kam (seedha pyramid). Par ek bade ped par bahut se keede aur pakshi rehte hain — neeche 1 ped, upar hazaaron jeev — pyramid ulta ho jata hai. (PYQ 2023-S2 Q20.)

## D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — pradushan aur food chain pehchano

1. **Pradushan ka prakar dekho** — hawa (CO, SO₂, NOx, PM2.5) / jal (industrial waste) / dhwani.
2. **Gas milao** — CO₂ = global warming; CFC = ozone chhed; CH₄ = biogas + greenhouse.
3. **Food chain banao** — utpadak → upbhokta → apghatak; har star par urja kam hoti hai.
