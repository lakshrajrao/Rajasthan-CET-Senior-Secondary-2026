## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — har darbar ki apni shaili

Rajasthan me kabhi ek rajya nahi tha — alag-alag Rajput darbar the: Mewar (Udaipur), Marwar (Jodhpur), Jaipur, Kota, Bundi, Kishangarh. Har darbar ke raja ne apne chitrakaron ko sanrakshan diya, aur har jagah chitrakala ka apna rang ban gaya. **Mewar ki shaili sabse purani** mani jati hai — usme Ramayana-Mahabharata aur Ragmala ke chitra bane. **Bundi** me hari-bhari prakriti chitrit hui, **Kota** me shikar ke drishya. **Kishangarh** me Nihal Chand ne Bani Thani banayi — use "Bharat ki Mona Lisa" kaha jata hai. **Jaipur** me Mughal darbar ka prabhav dikha. Inke alawa do vishesh paramparayein hain: **Phad** — Shahpura ki lambi scroll painting jisme Pabuji aur Devnarayan ki katha hoti hai — aur **Pichwai** — Nathdwara me Shri Krishna (Shrinathji) par aadharit chitra. Chhoti, bareek chitrakala ko **Miniature Painting** kehte hain.

## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — alag shailiyan kyun bani?

- **Har darbar swatantra tha.** Ek hi rajya nahi tha, isliye har raja ne apne pasand ke vishay aur apne kalakaron ko badhava diya — shailiyan alag hona swabhavik tha. (Yah ek samanya aitihasik karan hai, koi kalpanik katha nahi.)
- **Vishay darbar ki pasand se tay hota tha.** Mewar me dharmik granth (Ramayana, Ragmala), Kota me raja ka shikar ka shauk — vishay aur shaili ek dusre se jude hain.
- **Jaipur par Mughal prabhav kyun?** Jaipur ke Kachhwaha raja Mughal darbar se najdeek se jude the — isiliye unki chitrakala me Mughal rang dikhta hai. (Purane notes me yahi likha hai: "Mughal prabhav".)
- **Phad lambi scroll kyun hai?** Kyunki vah ek lambi katha sunane ke liye banti hai — Bhopa gayak katha sunate hue scroll ko kholta jata hai. Scroll ki lambai katha ki lambai se judi hai.

## D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — kisi shaili ko 3 steps me pehchano

1. **Vishay dekho.** Krishna/Shrinathji → Pichwai. Bani Thani (lambi gardan, badi aankhein) → Kishangarh. Shikar ka drishya → Kota. Lambi scroll par katha → Phad.
2. **Sthan dekho.** Udaipur → Mewar. Ajmer → Kishangarh. Shahpura (Bhilwara) → Phad. Nathdwara → Pichwai.
3. **Kalakar ka naam dekho.** Nihal Chand → Kishangarh (Bani Thani). Shree Lal Joshi → Phad painting.

## E. VISUAL MEMORY CONNECTION

## B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — pahad, ret aur pani par kile

Rajasthan ke kile sirf imaratein nahi hain — vah rajdhani, suraksha aur shakti ke kendra the. **Chittorgarh** sabse bada kila mana jata hai — usi me Vijay Stambh, Kirti Stambh aur Rani Padmini Mahal hain. **Kumbhalgarh** (Rajsamand) ki deewar duniya ki dusri sabse lambi deewar kahi jati hai (Great Wall ke baad) — aur vahi Maharana Pratap ka janm-sthan hai. **Jaisalmer ka Sonar Kila** peele balua patthar se bana hai aur **jeevant kila (living fort)** hai — uske andar aaj bhi log rehte hain. **Gagron** (Jhalawar) **jal-durg** hai — pani se ghira hua. **Mehrangarh** (Jodhpur) pahadi par hai, **Amer** (Jaipur) me Sheesh Mahal hai, **Lohagarh** (Bharatpur) mitti ka kila hai. Inme se **6 kilon** ko 2013 me **UNESCO World Heritage (Hill Forts of Rajasthan)** ghoshit kiya gaya. Iske alawa **Jantar Mantar, Jaipur (2010)** aur **Keoladeo National Park (1982)** bhi UNESCO suchi me hain.

## C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — kile pahadon par kyun bane?

- **Suraksha.** Uncha sthan — pahadi ya ret ke beech — dushman ke liye chadhna mushkil banata hai aur upar se door tak dikhai deta hai. Isiliye Mehrangarh pahadi par hai aur Kumbhalgarh Aravalli me.
- **Jaisalmer "jeevant kila" kyun hai?** Kyunki uske andar aaj bhi log rehte hain — dukanein, ghar, mandir sab kile ke andar hain. Zyadatar kile aaj khali smarak hain; Jaisalmer me jeevan chal raha hai. Isiliye use "living fort" kaha jata hai.
- **Gagron jal-durg kyun hai?** Vah nadiyon se ghira hua hai — pani hi uski suraksha-deewar hai. (Purane notes: "Jal-durg (pani se ghira)".)
- **UNESCO ne 6 kilon ko ek saath kyun chuna (2013)?** Kyunki vah ek hi parampara ke hain — Rajput pahadi kile (Hill Forts of Rajasthan) — isiliye unhe ek samuh (serial site) ke roop me suchi me dala gaya. (Yah UNESCO ke serial-site niyam ka samanya vivaran hai.)

## D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — kile ke sawal ko 4 steps me hal karo

2. **Visheshta puchho.** Sabse bada? Chittorgarh. Sabse lambi deewar? Kumbhalgarh. Jeevant kila? Jaisalmer. Jal-durg? Gagron. Sheesh Mahal? Amer. Mitti ka kila? Lohagarh.
3. **UNESCO?** 6 Hill Forts (2013) me hai ya nahi — upar ki list dekho.
4. **Stambh?** Vijay Stambh aur Kirti Stambh — donon Chittorgarh me hain (neeche §G dekho).
