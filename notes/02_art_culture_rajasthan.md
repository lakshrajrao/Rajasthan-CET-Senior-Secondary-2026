# 2. ART & CULTURE OF RAJASTHAN (कला और संस्कृति) — HIGH PRIORITY

## 2.1 Introduction
Rajasthan ki sanskriti — **rang, nritya-sangeet, chitrakala, hastshilp, mele-tyohar aur sahitya** ka sangam. Exam me: nritya, chitrakala shailiyan, hastshilp (GI tag), mele aur vastukala se direct factual questions aate hain.

## 2.2 Lok Nritya (Folk Dances)
| Nritya | Kshetra / Samuday | Visheshta |
|---|---|---|
| **Ghoomar** | Marwar / Rajput mahilayein | Rajasthan ka **rajya nritya**; ghaghra ghumate hue |
| **Kalbeliya** | Sapera samuday | **UNESCO Intangible Heritage 2010**; Gulabo Sapera ne prasiddh kiya |
| **Terah Taali** | Kamad samuday (Pali) | Shareer par 13 manjire bandhkar |

| **Chang Dhap** | Shekhawati (Holi) | Chang vadya ke saath |
| **Bhavai** | — | Sir par ghade rakhkar santulan |
| **Fire Dance (Agni Nritya)** | Bikaner (Jasnathi sampraday) | Aag par nritya |

## 2.3 Lok Sangeet aur Vadya
- **Manganiyar aur Langa** — prasiddh lok gayak samuday (Barmer-Jaisalmer).
- **Pabuji, Tejaji, Devnarayan** — lok devtaon ki kathayein (Phad gayaki).
- Vadya: Morchang, Khartal, Algoza, Ravanhatta, Sarangi, Dholak.

## 2.4 Chitrakala (Painting Styles)
| Shaili | Kshetra | Visheshta |
|---|---|---|
| **Mewar** | Udaipur | Ramayana-Mahabharata, Ragmala; prachinatam |
| **Marwar** | Jodhpur | — |
| **Bundi** | Bundi | Hara-bhara prakriti chitran |
| **Kota** | Kota | Shikar drishya |
| **Kishangarh** | Ajmer | **Bani Thani** — "Bharat ki Mona Lisa"; Nihal Chand ne banayi |
| **Jaipur** | Jaipur | Mughal prabhav |
| **Phad Painting** | Shahpura (Bhilwara) | Pabuji/Devnarayan ki lambi scroll katha; **Shree Lal Joshi** prasiddh |
| **Pichwai** | Nathdwara | Shri Krishna (Shrinathji) par aadharit |
| **Miniature Painting** | — | Chhoti, bareek chitrakala |

**Confusion:** Bani Thani = Kishangarh, Nihal Chand. Phad = Shahpura, Pabuji katha. Pichwai = Nathdwara, Krishna.
