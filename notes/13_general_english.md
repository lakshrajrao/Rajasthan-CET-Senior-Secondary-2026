# 13. GENERAL ENGLISH (सामान्य अंग्रेज़ी)

## 13.1 Parts of Speech & Tenses
- **8 Parts of Speech:** Noun, Pronoun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb, Preposition, Conjunction, Interjection.
- **Tenses (12):** Present/Past/Future × Simple/Continuous/Perfect/Perfect Continuous. **Solved:** "She has been reading since morning" — **Present Perfect Continuous**. "He went yesterday" — **Simple Past**.
- **Subject–Verb Agreement:** "Each of the students **is** present" (Each → singular). "Neither Ram nor Shyam **is** here."

## 13.2 Active–Passive, Direct–Indirect
- **Active → Passive:** "Ram writes a letter" → "A letter **is written** by Ram." "She ate an apple" → "An apple **was eaten** by her."
- **Direct → Indirect:** "He said, 'I am busy'" → "He said that he **was** busy." (Present → Past; 'said' ke baad 'that'.)

## 13.3 Vocabulary — Synonyms, Antonyms, One-Word Substitution (HAR SAAL)
| Word | Synonym | Antonym |
|---|---|---|
| Happy | Joyful, Glad | Sad |
| Brave | Courageous | Cowardly |
| Honest | Truthful | Dishonest |
| **Susceptible** | Vulnerable | **Resistant / Immune** (2024 PYQ) |
| Abandon | Leave, Forsake | Keep |
| Ancient | Old, Antique | Modern |

**One-Word Substitution (2024 PYQ-type):** "A person who loves himself" — **Narcissist**. "One who can use both hands" — **Ambidextrous**. "One who knows everything" — **Omniscient**. "One who can do anything" — **Omnipotent**. "Fear of water" — **Hydrophobia**. "A place where birds are kept" — **Aviary**. "One who writes books" — **Author**.

## 13.4 Articles, Prepositions, Error Spotting
- **Articles:** **A/An** (indefinite — ek), **The** (definite — nishchit). "An hour" (h silent), "A university" (u = 'yu' sound).
- **Prepositions:** in/on/at (time/place), to/for, by/with, since/for (since = point of time, for = duration). **Solved:** "He has been ill **since** Monday"; "… **for** three days."
- **Error spotting trick:** Subject–verb, article, preposition, tense — charon check karo; "Each/Every/Neither" → singular verb.

## 13.5 Reading Comprehension (2024 me chess passage aaya tha)
- **Trick:** Pehle questions padho, phir passage; pehla aur aakhri paragraph dhyaan se; option me passage ke shabd copy hone par bhi arth check karo — **exact wording ≠ correct answer**.

**English high-priority:** Tenses (12), Active–Passive formula, One-word (Narcissist, Omniscient, Ambidextrous), Susceptible–Resistant, Articles (An hour / A university), Since/For.
