# 6. ECONOMY OF RAJASTHAN (राजस्थान की अर्थव्यवस्था)

## 6.1 Overview
- **GSDP (2025-26 proj.):** ~₹21.52 lakh crore (Budget 2026-27 bhashan ke anusar, 2023-24 se 41.39% adhik — Indian Express, Feb 2026); **Per capita income ~₹2,02,349** (2025-26 proj.).
- **Budget 2026-27 (11 Feb 2026, Diya Kumari):** Kul aakar **₹6,10,956 crore**; Capital expenditure **₹53,978 crore**; Fiscal deficit ~3.69% of GSDP (₹79,492 cr); Revenue deficit ₹24,313 cr. Vision: **Viksit Rajasthan @2047**. 10 pillars — infrastructure, civic amenities, industry, HRD, social security, tourism/culture, governance/digital, agriculture, green growth, $4.3 trillion economy by 2047 (Indian Express/PTI, Feb 2026).
- **Budget 2026-27 ki ghoshnaein (yaad rakhne yogya):** Rajasthan State Testing Agency (RSTA) — NTA ki tarz par; Carbon Credit Pilot Project; Solar parks Bikaner & Jaisalmer (4,830 MW, JV); 42,000 km sadkein; Mukhyamantri Yuva Swarojgar Yojana — 1 lakh yuva, ₹10 lakh tak loan par 100% byaj subsidy, ₹1,000 cr; Lakhpati Didi loan limit ₹1 lakh → ₹1.5 lakh; PM-KUSUM — 50,000 solar pump; Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya ~₹3,000 cr; Thar Cultural Circuit ₹5,000 cr; CM Jal Jeevan Mission ₹6,800 cr.

## 6.3 Industry & Minerals
- **Pradhan udyog:** Cement (Chittorgarh — Bharat me agrani), Textile (Bhilwara — "Manchester of Rajasthan"; Pali), Marble/Granite, Zinc smelting (Chanderiya, Chittorgarh — Hindustan Zinc), Salt (Sambhar), Glass, Gems & Jewellery (Jaipur).
- **RIICO** — Rajasthan State Industrial Development & Investment Corporation; **Rajasthan Industrial Policy**.
- **Khanij:** Zinc-Lead (Zawar — Bharat me sabse bada), Marble, Limestone, Gypsum, **Petroleum — Barmer (Mangala, Bhagyam, Aishwariya fields; Cairn/Vedanta)**; Jaisalmer — natural gas.
- **Solar:** Bharat me **No.1** — Bhadla (Jodhpur) mega solar park; RE-SAF National Award Nov 2026 (VP C.P. Radhakrishnan dwara pradan — ET Rajasthan, 6 Nov 2026).
