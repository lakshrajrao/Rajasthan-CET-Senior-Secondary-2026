- **73rd Amendment (1992, lagoo 24 Apr 1993)** — Panchayati Raj ko samvaidhanik darja; **11th Schedule** (29 vishay).

- **Gram Sabha:** gaon ke sabhi matdata; **Sarpanch** — Gram Panchayat ka pramukh; **Pradhan** — Panchayat Samiti; **Zila Pramukh** — Zila Parishad.
