### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — ek desh, ek niyam-pustak

26 January 1950 se pehle Bharat ke paas apna koi Samvidhan nahi tha — desh British kaanoon (Government of India Act, 1935) se chal raha tha. Aazadi ke baad Samvidhan Sabha ne lagbhag 3 saal me ek niyam-pustak likhi: **26 November 1949 ko apnayi gayi, 26 January 1950 ko lagoo hui**. Is pustak ke pehle page par **Prastavana** hai — "We the People of India" — jo batati hai ki shakti janta se aati hai.

Pustak ke teen bade hisse yaad rakho. **Part III (Art 12–35)** — Moolik Adhikar: ye wo 6 adhikar hain jo har nagrik ko mile aur jinhe nyayalay me lagoo karaya ja sakta hai. **Part IV (Art 36–51)** — DPSP: ye rajya ke liye nirdesh hain — sarkar ko kya karna chahiye (jaise Panchayati Raj, Art 40) — lekin inhe court me lagoo nahi karaya ja sakta. **Part IV-A (Art 51A)** — Moolik Kartavya: 1976 me jode gaye, nagrik ke farz.

Samvidhan badalta bhi rehta hai — **sanshodhan (amendment)** se. Kuch sanshodhan itne bade the ki unhone Samvidhan ka chehra badal diya: 42nd (1976) ko "Mini Constitution" kaha jata hai; 44th (1978) ne sampatti ke adhikar ko Moolik Adhikaron se hata diya.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — ye vyavastha aisi kyun hai?

- **Moolik Adhikar nyayalay me lagoo kyun hote hain, DPSP kyun nahi?** Adhikar vyakti ki swatantrata ki raksha karte hain — agar sarkar unhe chheene, to nagrik court ja sakta hai (Art 32). DPSP **lakshya** hain — jaise "sabko shiksha" ya "gaay vadh par rok" — inhe poora karne ke liye paisa, samay aur kaanoon chahiye; court sarkar ko budget kharch karne ka aadesh nahi de sakta. Isliye DPSP "nirdeshak" hain, "lagoo karne yogya" nahi.
- **42nd Amendment ko "Mini Constitution" kyun kaha jata hai?** 1976 me isne ek saath bahut kuch badla — Prastavana me 3 shabd jode (Socialist, Secular, Integrity), Moolik Kartavya jode, DPSP ko FR se upar rakha. Itne badlav ek saath isliye ise "chhota Samvidhan" kaha gaya.
- **Sampatti ka adhikar FR se kyun hataya gaya (44th, 1978)?** Is adhikar ke karan sarkar ki bhoomi-sudhar (land reform) yojanayein court me atak jati thi — garibon ko zameen dene wale kaanoon par sampatti ke adhikar ka hawala dekar rok lag jati thi. Isliye sanshodhan se sampatti ka adhikar FR se hata kar **Art 300A** me ek samanya kaanooni adhikar bana diya gaya.
- **Matdan ki aayu 21 se 18 kyun ki gayi (61st, 1989)?** Iske peeche soch thi ki 18 saal ka yuva — jo kaanooni roop se vayask mana jata hai — desh ke faislon me bhi hissa le sakta hai. (Ye ek neeti-nirnay hai, koi prakritik niyam nahi — exam me sirf tathya poochha jata hai: kaun sa sanshodhan, kis saal.)

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — sanshodhan ke sawal ko 4 steps me hal karo

1. **Saal dekho** — sanshodhan ka saal yaad karo (1976, 1978, 1985, 1989, 1992, 2002, 2016, 2019, 2023).
2. **Kya badla?** — Prastavana? Adhikar? Matdan? Panchayat? GST?
3. **Number jodo** — saal + badlav = sanshodhan number (neeche §F ki table se).
4. **Confusion check** — 42nd vs 44th (dono 1970s, ulta asar: 42nd ne joda, 44th ne hataya); 73rd vs 74th (Panchayat vs Nagarpalika).

### E. VISUAL MEMORY CONNECTION

Samvidhan ko ek **bade parivar ki niyam-pustak** samjho. Prastavana = parivar ka sankalp ("hum sab milkar rahenge"). Moolik Adhikar = har sadasya ke **pakke adhikar** (koi chheen nahi sakta). DPSP = **ghar ke lakshya** ("sab padhenge, sab swasth rahenge") — achhe iraade, lekin in par court nahi ja sakte. Kartavya = har sadasya ke **farz**. Aur sanshodhan = jab parivar bada ho jaye to niyam-pustak me **naye page jodna**.

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — sarkar kaise banti hai

Har 5 saal me janta (18 saal se upar ke matdata) **Lok Sabha** ke 543 sadasya chunti hai. Jis dal ya gathbandhan ko bahumat milta hai, uska neta **Pradhan Mantri** banta hai aur apna **Mantrimandal (Council of Ministers)** banata hai — yehi log desh ki sarkar chalate hain. **Rashtrapati** desh ke samvaidhanik pramukh hain: unke naam par sarkar chalti hai, lekin asal faisle Mantrimandal leta hai aur Rashtrapati unki salah par kaam karte hain.

Sansad ke do sadan hain. **Lok Sabha** — janta ka sadan, seedhe chuna jata hai, 543 seats. **Rajya Sabha** — rajyon ka sadan, 245 seats — iske sadasyon ko rajyon ki Vidhan Sabhayein chunti hain. Rajasthan se Lok Sabha me **25** aur Rajya Sabha me **10** sadasya jate hain. Dono sadan milkar kaanoon banate hain; paisa (money bill) sirf Lok Sabha se shuru hota hai.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — do sadan kyun, ek kyun nahi?

- **Rajya Sabha kyun hai?** Bharat ek **sangh (federation)** hai — rajyon ko bhi kendra ke faislon me awaaz chahiye. Rajya Sabha me har rajya ke pratinidhi baithe hain, isliye kendra koi kaanoon rajyon par bina sune nahi thop sakta. (Ye ek sanrachna ka kaaran hai — samvidhan nirmataon ka design — koi prakritik niyam nahi.)
- **Rashtrapati seedhe kyun nahi chune jate?** Rashtrapati ko Sansad aur rajyon ki Vidhan Sabhayein milkar chunti hain (electoral college). Iska kaaran: Rashtrapati **samvaidhanik** pramukh hain, rajnaitik nahi — unhe janta se seedha janadesh nahi chahiye, warna PM se shakti ki ladai ho sakti hai.
- **PM ko Lok Sabha ka bahumat kyun chahiye?** Kyunki sarkar Lok Sabha ke prati **jawabdeh (accountable)** hai — agar bahumat kho de, to sarkar gir jati hai. Ye sansadiya vyavastha ka mool siddhant hai.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — kaanoon kaise banta hai (bill → Act)

1. **Bill pesh hota hai** — kisi ek sadan me (money bill sirf Lok Sabha me).
2. **Dono sadan pass karte hain** — bahas, sanshodhan, matdan.
3. **Rashtrapati ke paas jata hai** — assent (manzoori) milne par bill **Act (kaanoon)** ban jata hai.
4. **Exam me poochha jata hai:** kaun sa sadan, kitni seats, kaun pramukh — isliye §F ki seat-table yaad rakho, bill ki gehrai nahi.

### E. VISUAL MEMORY CONNECTION

Sansad ko ek **do-manzila makan** samjho. **Neeche ki manzil (Lok Sabha)** — janta seedhe andar aati hai, 543 kursi. **Upar ki manzil (Rajya Sabha)** — rajyon ke pratinidhi baithe hain, 245 kursi. Makan ka **malik (Rashtrapati)** upar rehta hai aur makan ke kaagaz par hastakshar karta hai — lekin makan ka kaam **chabiwala (PM + Mantrimandal)** chalata hai, jo neeche ki manzil ke bahumat se chuna jata hai.

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — jab adhikar chheena jaye to kahan jayein?

Maan lo kisi nagrik ko lagta hai ki sarkar ne uska Moolik Adhikar chheen liya — jaise use bina kaaran giraftar kar liya gaya. Wo seedhe **Supreme Court** ja sakta hai — **Art 32** ke tahat. Ambedkar ne Art 32 ko "Samvidhan ki aatma" isiliye kaha: adhikar kagaz par hone se kuch nahi hota, unhe lagoo karane ka rasta bhi chahiye — aur wo rasta Art 32 hai.

Supreme Court Bharat ki **sabse badi adalat** hai. Iske faisle pure desh par lagoo hote hain. Agar koi kaanoon Samvidhan ke khilaf hai, to SC use **asamvaidhanik** ghoshit karke radd kar sakta hai — ise **nyayik punaravalokan (judicial review)** kehte hain. Court ke pramukh **Mukhya Nyayadhish (Chief Justice of India, CJI)** hote hain.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — SC ko itni shakti kyun?

- **Art 32 seedhe SC jaane ka adhikar kyun deta hai?** Kyunki Moolik Adhikar bina upchar ke bekaar hain. Agar nagrik ko pehle zila court, phir HC, phir SC jaana padta, to nyay me saal lag jate. Isliye Samvidhan ne seedha rasta diya — aur Art 32 khud ek **Moolik Adhikar** hai (isliye Ambedkar ne ise "aatma" kaha).
- **Judicial review kyun zaroori hai?** Sansad kaanoon banati hai, lekin Samvidhan sabse upar hai. Agar Sansad Samvidhan ke khilaf kaanoon bana de, to koi to hona chahiye jo use roke — ye kaam SC karta hai. Isi se **Samvidhan ki sarvochchata** banti rehti hai.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — ek case SC tak kaise pahunchta hai

1. **Seedha rasta (Art 32):** Moolik Adhikar ka mamla — nagrik seedhe SC me writ (rit) dayar karta hai.
2. **Appeal ka rasta:** Zila court → High Court → Supreme Court (neeche ki adalat ke faisle ke khilaf appeal).
3. **Writ (rit) ke 5 prakar** (Art 32 ke tahat SC jari karta hai): **Habeas Corpus** (vyakti ko pesh karo), **Mandamus** (kaam karne ka aadesh), **Prohibition** (neeche ki adalat ko rokna), **Certiorari** (mamla upar mangana), **Quo Warranto** (pad par adhikar poochhna).
4. **Faisla:** SC ka faisla antim hota hai aur pure desh par lagoo hota hai.

Umpire (SC) niyam-pustak dekh kar faisla karta hai: "Ye shot niyam ke khilaf hai — radd." Umpire khud khel nahi khelta, lekin uske faisle par khel chalta hai.

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — sarkar par nazar kaun rakhta hai?

Sarkar kaam kare, ye kaafi nahi — us par **nazar** bhi rakhni hoti hai. Isi liye alag-alag **aayog (commissions)** bane hain, jo sarkar se swatantra hokar kaam karte hain.

**State Information Commission (SIC)** RTI Act, 2005 ke tahat bana hai. Agar koi nagrik sarkar se soochna mange aur use na mile, to wo SIC me shikayat kar sakta hai. SIC ka kaam hai soochna dilana.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — alag aayog kyun chahiye?

- **Chunaav ke liye alag SEC kyun?** Panchayat/Nagarpalika ke chunaav pure rajya me ek saath, hazaron gaon-shaharon me hote hain — inhe kendriya EC se karana vyavharik nahi hota. Isliye Samvidhan (Art 243K) ne har rajya me alag SEC banaya.
- **SIC kyun?** RTI kaanoon ne nagrik ko soochna ka adhikar diya, lekin adhikar tabhi kaam karta hai jab use na milne par koi sunwai ho. SIC wahi sunwai karta hai — bina SIC ke RTI sirf kagaz par rehta.
- **SHRC kyun?** Manav adhikar ka ullanghan aksar sarkari tantra se hota hai — isliye jaanch ke liye sarkar se **alag** sanstha chahiye. SHRC ki sifarishein sarkar ko manni nahi padti (yeh ek kamzori bhi mani jati hai), lekin iski jaanch aur report dabav banati hai.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — ek shikayat ka rasta

1. **RTI shikayat (SIC):** Nagrik soochna mange → vibhag na de ya deri kare → SIC me appeal → SIC soochna dilane ka aadesh de sakta hai.
2. **Manav adhikar shikayat (SHRC):** Koi vyakti SHRC me shikayat kare → SHRC jaanch kare → sarkar ko sifarish bheje.
3. **Zila prashasan:** Collector — jile ke sabhi vibhagon ka samanvay, kaanoon-vyavastha, chunaav me sahyog. SP — police vyavastha ke pramukh.

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — gaon ki sarkar

1959 se pehle gaon ka faisla upar se hota tha — zila ya rajya se. **2 October 1959** ko **Nagaur (Rajasthan)** me Pradhan Mantri Nehru ne pehli baar **Panchayati Raj** ka udghatan kiya — Bharat ka pehla rajya jahan gaon ko apni sarkar mili. Shuruaat me ye sirf ek yojana thi, kaanoon nahi. **73rd Amendment (1992, lagoo 24 Apr 1993)** ne ise **Samvidhan me darja** de diya — ab har rajya ko Panchayati Raj rakhna hi hoga, chunaav har 5 saal me hone hi honge. Sheharon ke liye wahi kaam **74th Amendment** ne kiya — **Nagarpalika** (Nagar Nigam/Nagar Palika) ko samvaidhanik darja.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — teen star kyun, ek kyun nahi?

- **Teen star kyun chahiye?** Gaon ke faisle (sadak, talab) gaon star par, block ke faisle (kai gaon ke school, swasthya kendra) block star par, aur zila star ke faisle (zila ki yojana) zila star par — har star apne dayre ke faisle leta hai. Ek hi star hota to ya to gaon ki awaaz upar nahi pahunchti, ya zila ke faisle gaon par thop diye jate.
- **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957) ne 3-tier kyun sujhaya?** Is samiti ne kaha: loktantra gaon tak pahunche, iske liye shakti ko **vikendrikrit (decentralise)** karna hoga — upar se neeche nahi, neeche se upar. Isi sifarish par 1959 me Nagaur me shuruaat hui.
- **Mahila aarakshan kyun?** Panchayaton me mahilaon ki hissedari ke bina aadhi aabadi faislon se bahar rehti thi. Samvidhan ne **1/3** aarakshan diya; Rajasthan me ye **50%** hai (⚠️ verify current — purane notes me pehle se flag tha).

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — Panchayati Raj, step by step

1. **Gram Sabha** — gaon ke sabhi matdata; saal me kam se kam 2 baithak (⚠️ quorum ki sankhya purane notes me nahi hai — 2024-P3 Q7 "Gram Sabha quorum" par PYQ aaya tha; quorum ka aankda exam se pehle verify karo).
2. **Gram Panchayat** — Sarpanch ke netritva me, gaon ke kaam (sadak, pani, safai).
3. **Panchayat Samiti** — Pradhan ke netritva me, block star (kai gaon).
4. **Zila Parishad** — Zila Pramukh ke netritva me, zila star (yojana, samanvay).
5. **Chunaav har 5 saal** me; **11th Schedule** ke 29 vishay Panchayaton ko diye gaye; **12th Schedule** ke 18 vishay Nagarpalika ko.
