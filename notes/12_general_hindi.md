## Chapter 1 — Sangya, Ling aur Vachan

**Official topic:** sangya. **Bank MCQ:** `12_GENERAL_HINDI-013` (Pustak ka ling). **PYQ:** 2024-P1 Q12 (Sangya – jativachak).

### A. VISUAL INTRODUCTION — sangya ke 5 prakar, ek chhoti kahani me

```
Ek gaon me ek LADKA (jativachak) rehta tha.
Uska naam RAM (vyaktivachak) tha.
Uske paas SONA (dravyavachak) nahi tha, par
uski IMAANDARI (bhavvachak) mashhoor thi.
Gaon ki SENA (samuhvachak) uski izzat karti thi.
         ▲                 ▲
   naam dene wala      gun/bhav dikhane wala
   shabd = SANGYA      bhi SANGYA hai
```

> **How to use this diagram:** Cover the labels in brackets and ask for each word: ye kis prakar ki sangya hai, aur kyun? That is retrieval practice, not reading.

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — naam dene wala shabd

Har cheez ka koi naam hota hai — aadmi ka, jagah ka, cheez ka, bhavna ka. Jis shabd se kisi **vyakti, vastu, sthan, samuh ya bhav** ka naam pata chale, use **sangya** kehte hain. "Ram" ek vyakti ka naam hai, "Jaipur" ek sthan ka, "sena" ek samuh ka, "imaandari" ek bhav ka. Sangya ke saath hi do aur baatein judi hain: **ling** (pulling ya stree-ling — shabd mardana hai ya zanana) aur **vachan** (ekvachan ya bahuvachan — ek hai ya anek). Hindi me har sangya ka ling tay hota hai — 'pustak' stree-ling hai, 'granth' pulling — aur exam me yahi poochha jata hai.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — sangya ke prakar kyun bane?

- **Kyun 5 prakar?** Kyunki naam alag-alag cheezon ke hote hain: kisi **ek khaas** cheez ka (vyaktivachak — Ram, Jaipur), kisi **poori jaati** ka (jativachak — ladka, nagar), kisi **gun/bhav** ka (bhavvachak — imaandari, sukh), kisi **samuh** ka (samuhvachak — sena, bheed), kisi **padarth** ka (dravyavachak — sona, doodh, paani). Prakar ka sawaal yahi hai: naam kis *tarah* ki cheez ka hai?
- **Ling kyun tay hai?** Hindi me ling ek **vyakaran ki parampara (convention)** hai — koi gehra tark nahi hai ki 'pustak' stree-ling kyun hai. Ise yaad karna padta hai. (Methodology rule: jab honest answer "convention" ho, to wahi likho — koi nakli karan mat banao.)
- **Vachan kyun badalta hai?** Ek se anek hone par shabd ka roop badalta hai (ladka → ladke, kitab → kitabein) — kyunki vakya me kriya aur visheshan bhi vachan se milte hain ("ladka **jata hai**" / "ladke **jate hain**").

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — sangya pehchanne ke 3 steps

1. **Shabd kiska naam hai?** Vyakti/jagah → vyaktivachak; poori jaati → jativachak; gun/bhav → bhavvachak; samuh → samuhvachak; padarth → dravyavachak.
2. **Ling poochha gaya hai?** 'Pustak' jaisi yaad karne wali sangyaon ko alag se yaad rakho — ant (ending) se ling tay nahi hota (neeche §G dekho).
3. **Vachan badlo:** ekvachan → bahuvachan me roop badalta hai; kriya bhi usi ke anusar badalti hai.

### E. VISUAL MEMORY CONNECTION

Picture a **naam-patti (nameplate)** on five doors: pehle darwaze par ek vyakti ka naam (RAM), dusre par ek jaati ka board (LADKA), teesre par ek bhav ka poster (IMAANDARI), chauthe par ek bheed ki tasveer (SENA), paanchve par ek dher sona (SONA). Har darwaza ek prakar hai — darwaza kholo, naam dekho, prakar bolo.

## Chapter 2 — Sarvanam

**Official topic:** sarvanam. **Bank MCQs:** none for this chapter (bank 001–020 cover other topics). **PYQ:** 2024-P1 Q11 (Sarvanam – anya purush).

### A. VISUAL INTRODUCTION — sangya ki jagah lene wala shabd

```
RAM ne kaha: "RAM ko paani chahiye. RAM ko bhookh lagi hai."
  (baat to sahi, par kitni ajeeb lagti hai!)

RAM ne kaha: "MUJHE paani chahiye. MUJHE bhookh lagi hai."
  (ab baat sahi lagti hai — MUJHE = sarvanam)

SANGYA (Ram) ──► SARVANAM (main, mujhe, mera)
  naam baar-baar   naam ki jagah
  lena ajeeb       lene wala shabd
```

> **How to use this diagram:** Cover the second box and try saying the first sentence with "Ram" three times — feel why the language needs sarvanam. That is the WHY, not a rule to memorise.

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — naam ki jagah lene wala

Sochiye aap kisi ko apne dost ke baare me bata rahe hain. "Ram aaya. Ram ne khana khaya. Ram ne mujhe bulaya. Ram achha ladka hai." — Ram, Ram, Ram — sunne me kitna ajeeb lagta hai! Isi liye bhasha me **sarvanam** hai: sangya ki jagah lene wala shabd. "Ram aaya. **Usne** khana khaya. **Usne** mujhe bulaya. **Wah** achha ladka hai." — 'usne', 'wah', 'mujhe' — ye sab sarvanam hain. Jo shabd **sangya ke badle** aaye, use sarvanam kehte hain. Iske 6 prakar hain — aur exam me sabse zyada **purushvachak** (main/tum/vah) aur **sambandhvachak** (jo/so) poochhe jate hain.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — sarvanam kyun chahiye?

- **Kyun chahiye?** Bina sarvanam ke har baar pura naam lena padta — baat lambi, bhari aur ajeeb ho jati. Sarvanam bhasha ko **chhota aur swabhavik** banata hai. Ye vyakaran ka niyam nahi, bhasha ki zaroorat hai.
- **Purush kyun 3?** Bolne wala (uttam — main/ham), sunne wala (madhyam — tu/tum/aap), aur jiske baare me baat ho (anya — vah/ve, yah/ye). Har baatchit me ye teen sthitiyaan hoti hain — isliye 3 purush.
- **'Vah' kab sarvanam, kab visheshan?** Jab 'vah' akela sangya ki jagah le ("vah aaya") to sarvanam; jab 'vah' kisi sangya ke saath uski visheshta bataye ("vah ladka aaya") to visheshan. Ek hi shabd, do kaam — kaam se pehchano, shabd se nahi.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — sarvanam pehchanne ke 3 steps

1. **Shabd sangya ki jagah aaya hai?** "Ram ki jagah 'usne'" — haan, to sarvanam hai.
2. **Kaun bol raha hai / kiske baare me?** Bolne wala → uttam; sunne wala → madhyam; teesra → anya.
3. **Sambandh ya sawaal hai?** "Jo... so..." (sambandh) → sambandhvachak; "kaun/kya" (sawaal) → prashnavachak.

### E. VISUAL MEMORY CONNECTION

Picture **teen kursi** — pehli par aap baithe hain (MAIN — uttam purush), dusri par saamne wala (TUM — madhyam purush), teesri par woh vyakti jiske baare me baat ho rahi hai (VAH — anya purush). Har sarvanam inhi teen kursiyon me se ek par baithta hai.

### F. IMPORTANT FACTS

| Sarvanam prakar | Pehchan | Udaharan |
|---|---|---|
| **Purushvachak** | Bolne/sunne/baat wale vyakti ke liye | Uttam: main, ham, mujhe, mera · Madhyam: tu, tum, aap, tumhara · Anya: vah, ve, yah, ye, usne |
| **Nishchayvachak** | Kisi nishchit cheez/vyakti ki ore ishara | yah, vah, ye, ve ("yah kitab meri hai") |
| **Anishchayvachak** | Anishchit (pata nahi kaun/kya) | koi, kuchh ("koi aaya tha") |
| **Sambandhvachak** | Do vakyon/shabdon ko jode | jo, so ("**jo** padhega, **vah** safal hoga") |
| **Prashnavachak** | Sawaal poochhe | kaun, kya, kab, kahan |
| **Nijvachak** | Khud ke liye (self) | swayam, khud, aap ("main **swayam** jaunga") |

### G. COMMON CONFUSIONS

| Confusion | Correct difference |
|---|---|
| 'Vah' — sarvanam ya visheshan? | **Kaam se pehchano (counter-example):** "vah aaya" — 'vah' akela sangya ki jagah → **sarvanam**. "vah ladka aaya" — 'vah' ladka ki visheshta batata hai → **visheshan**. Ek hi shabd, dono kaam. |
| 'Aap' — madhyam ya nijvachak? | **Dono, sandarbh se:** "aap aaiye" (sunne wale ke liye, samman) → madhyam purush. "Main aap chala jaunga" (khud) → nijvachak. |
| 'Jo' aur 'vah' dono sarvanam? | Haan, par alag prakar: 'jo' = **sambandhvachak** (jodta hai), 'vah' = **purushvachak** (anya purush). Mock P1 Q10 me yahi trap tha — stem ab poochhta hai "'jo' kis prakar ka sarvanam hai?" (sambandhvachak). |

## Chapter 3 — Visheshan

**Official topic:** visheshan. **Bank MCQs:** none for this chapter. **PYQs:** none for visheshan in the dataset (20 Hindi PYQs cover other topics — see Chapter map).

### A. VISUAL INTRODUCTION — sangya ki visheshta batane wala shabd

```
LADKA            →   ACHCHHA ladka      (kaisa? → gun)
LADKA            →   DO ladke           (kitne? → sankhya)
PAANI            →   THODA paani        (kitna? → pariman)
LADKA            →   VAH ladka          (kaun sa? → sanket)

   VISHESHAN = sangya ke baare me
   "kaisa / kitna / kaun sa" batane wala shabd
   (visheshan hamesha kisi SANGYA ke saath hota hai)
```

> **How to use this diagram:** Cover the right column and ask for each question (kaisa? kitne? kitna? kaun sa?) — the answer word is the visheshan, and the question tells you its type.

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — "kaisa ladka?"

Sochiye aap kisi ko bata rahe hain: "Ek ladka aaya." Sunne wala poochhega — "kaisa ladka?" Ab aap kehte hain: "Achchha ladka", "lamba ladka", "do ladke", "vah ladka". 'Achchha', 'lamba', 'do', 'vah' — ye sab **visheshan** hain: sangya ki **visheshta** (gun, sankhya, pariman, sanket) batane wale shabd. Visheshan akela nahi rehta — hamesha kisi sangya ke saath hota hai. 'Achchha' akela kuchh nahi kehta; 'achchha ladka' kehte hi tasveer saaf ho jati hai.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — visheshan kyun chahiye?

- **Kyun chahiye?** Bina visheshan ke har sangya ek jaisi lagti hai — "ladka", "paani", "kitab". Visheshan batata hai **kaun sa, kaisa, kitna** — bina iske baat adhuri rehti hai. Ye bhasha ki zaroorat hai, vyakaran ka niyam nahi.
- **Visheshan sangya ke anusar kyun badalta hai?** Hindi me visheshan sangya ke **ling aur vachan** se milta hai: "achchha ladka" (pulling) / "achchhi ladki" (stree-ling); "achchhe ladke" (bahuvachan). Ye milaan (agreement) vakya ko sahi banata hai — isiliye exam me "achchha ladki" jaisi galti pakdi jati hai.
- **Kyun 4 prakar?** Visheshta 4 tarah ki hoti hai: **gun** (kaisa), **sankhya** (kitne), **pariman** (kitna), **sanket** (kaun sa). Har sawaal ek prakar hai.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — visheshan pehchanne ke 3 steps

1. **Shabd kis sangya ke saath hai?** Sangya ke bina visheshan nahi hota — pehle sangya dhoondho.
2. **Kya batata hai?** Kaisa (gun) / kitne (sankhya) / kitna (pariman) / kaun sa (sanket).
3. **Ling–vachan milao:** visheshan ka roop sangya ke ling–vachan se milna chahiye ("achchhi ladki", "achchhe ladke").

### E. VISUAL MEMORY CONNECTION

Picture a **sangya par chipka hua rang-biranga sticker**: sangya ek saada dibba hai (LADKA), aur visheshan us par laga sticker hai (ACHCHHA / DO / VAH). Sticker akela kuchh nahi — dibbe par lagte hi dibba pehchan me aa jata hai.

### F. IMPORTANT FACTS

| Visheshan prakar | Sawaal | Udaharan |
|---|---|---|

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — hamesha ek jaisa rehne wala shabd

Hindi ke zyadatar shabd badalte rehte hain: 'ladka' 'ladke' ban jata hai, 'main' 'mujhe' ban jata hai, 'achchha' 'achchhi' ban jata hai. Par kuchh shabd **kabhi nahi badalte** — chahe ladka ho ya ladki, ek ho ya anek, aaj ho ya kal: 'dheere', 'aaj', 'yahan', 'aur', 'lekin', 'are!' — ye sab **avyaya** hain. 'Avyaya' ka matlab hi hai: **vyaya (badlav) nahi hone wala**. Avyaya 4 prakar ke hote hain: kriya ki visheshta batane wala (kriya-visheshan), sambandh batane wala (sambandhbodhak), jodne wala (samuchchaybodhak), aur bhav prakat karne wala (vismayadibodhak).

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — avyaya kyun nahi badalta?

- **Kyun nahi badalta?** Kyunki avyaya kisi **sangya se juda nahi hota** — ye kriya, vakya ya poori baat se juda hota hai. Ling aur vachan sangya ke gun hain; avyaya sangya nahi hai, isliye us par ling–vachan ka niyam lagta hi nahi. (Ye ek vyakaran ki rachna hai — convention, koi gehra karan nahi.)
- **Kyun 4 prakar?** Avyaya 4 kaam karta hai: **kriya** ki visheshta batana (dheere chalo), **sambandh** batana (ke saath), **jodna** (aur, lekin), **bhav** prakat karna (are!, wah!). Har kaam ek prakar hai.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — avyaya pehchanne ke 3 steps

1. **Shabd badalta hai ya nahi?** Ling/vachan badalkar dekho — badla nahi, to avyaya ho sakta hai.
2. **Kis se juda hai?** Kriya se (dheere) → kriya-visheshan; sambandh se (ke saath) → sambandhbodhak; jodne ka kaam (aur) → samuchchaybodhak; bhav (are!) → vismayadibodhak.
3. **Sangya to nahi?** Agar shabd kisi sangya ki visheshta bata raha hai, to visheshan hai (Chapter 3) — avyaya nahi.

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — "main gaya, main jata hoon, main jaunga"

Sochiye aap apne din ke baare me bata rahe hain. "Kal main school **gaya**" — kaam beeta hua hai. "Aaj main school **jata hoon**" — kaam chal raha hai. "Kal main school **jaunga**" — kaam aane wala hai. 'Gaya', 'jata hoon', 'jaunga' — teeno ek hi kriya 'jana' ke roop hain, bas **samay** alag hai. ### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — kaal kyun 3 hain?

- **Kyun 3 kaal?** Kyunki samay ki 3 hi sthitiyaan hain: jo **beet gaya**, jo **chal raha hai**, jo **aane wala hai**. Har bhasha me kaal hota hai — bina kaal ke aap bata hi nahi sakte kaam kab hua.
- **Sakarmak vs akarmak kyun?** Kyunki kuchh kaam ka asar kisi cheez par padta hai ("kitab padhna" — kitab par asar), aur kuchh kaam ka asar sirf karne wale par ("sona" — koi cheez nahi). 'Karm' (object) hai ya nahi — yahi antar hai.
- **Prernarthak kyun?** Kyunki kabhi kaam khud nahi karte, **kisi se karwate** hain ("likhwana", "karwana"). 'Va'/'wa' lagakar kriya se prernarthak banta hai — ye Hindi ki khaasiyat hai.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — kriya aur kaal pehchanne ke 3 steps

1. **Kaam wala shabd dhoondho** — jana, khana, padhna, sona — ye kriya hai.
2. **Karm hai ya nahi?** "Kya/ kise" ka jawab mile → sakarmak ("kitab padhta hai"); na mile → akarmak ("sota hai"). 'Va/wa' laga hai → prernarthak ("likhwaya").
3. **Samay poochho:** beeta → bhoot; chal raha → vartaman; aane wala → bhavishya. "Main jata hoon" — chal raha hai → **vartaman**.

## Chapter 6 — Varnamala aur Sandhi (sandhi-vichhed)

**Official topic:** sandhi & sandhi-vichhed. **Bank MCQs:** `12_GENERAL_HINDI-001` (Vidyalaya me sandhi), `12_GENERAL_HINDI-002` (Mahatma ka sandhi-vichhed). **PYQs:** 2024-P1 Q1 (Sandhi – Anvikshan); 2024-P1 Q2 (Sandhi absence); 2024-P4 Q10 (Sandhi-vicched – Aachchhadan).

### A. VISUAL INTRODUCTION — do dhwaniyan milkar ek nayi dhwani

```
  अ + आ = आ        विद्या + आलय = विद्यालय
  अ + इ = ए        नर + इन्द्र = नरेन्द्र
  अ + उ = ओ        महा + उत्सव = महोत्सव
  इ + अ = य        अति + अन्त = अत्यन्त
  ए + अ = अय्      ने + अन = नयन

   SANDHI = do varnon ke milne par hone wala badlav
   VICHED = mile hue shabd ko wapas todna (ulta kaam)
```

> **How to use this diagram:** Cover the right column and join each pair yourself, then check. Then cover the left column and split each joined word back. Joining = sandhi; splitting = vichhed.

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — bolte-bolte shabd jud jate hain

Sochiye aap tez bol rahe hain: "vidya aalay" — bolte-bolte ye "vidyaalay" ho jata hai, phir "vidyalaya". Do dhwaniyan paas aakar mil jati hain aur ek nayi dhwani ban jati hai — isi ko **sandhi** kehte hain. Ulta kaam — mile hue shabd ko wapas todna — **sandhi-vichhed** hai: "vidyalaya = vidya + aalay". Sandhi 3 prakar ki hai: **swar sandhi** (do swar milen), **vyanjan sandhi** (vyanjan se judav), **visarg sandhi** (visarg : se judav). Isse pehle varnamala yaad rakho: **11 swar** (अ, आ, इ, ई, उ, ऊ, ऋ, ए, ऐ, ओ, औ), **33 vyanjan** (क–ह), aur **anusvar (ं), anunasik (ँ), visarg (ः)**.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — sandhi kyun hoti hai?

- **Kyun hoti hai?** Bolne me suvidha ke liye. Do dhwaniyan paas aane par jeebh (tongue) ek hi baar me dono ko bolne ki koshish karti hai — aur beech me ek nayi dhwani ban jati hai. Ye bhasha ki **swabhavik pravritti** hai, koi banaya hua niyam nahi. Niyam is pravritti ko likhne ka tareeka hai.
- **Kyun 3 prakar?** Kyunki milne wali dhwaniyan 3 tarah ki hain: **swar + swar** (swar sandhi), **vyanjan se judav** (vyanjan sandhi), **visarg (:) se judav** (visarg sandhi). Pehle ye pehchano ki *kya mil raha hai* — prakar khud mil jayega.
- **Vichhed kyun poochha jata hai?** Kyunki vichhed jaanchta hai ki aapne sandhi ko *samjha* hai ya sirf rat liya hai — jodne wala tod bhi sakta hai.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — sandhi karne ke 4 steps

1. **Dono shabd todho:** pehle shabd ka antim varn + dusre shabd ka pehla varn dekho.
2. **Dono swar hain?** Swar sandhi — kaun sa niyam: dirgha (समान स्वर — अ+अ=आ), gun (अ+इ=ए, अ+उ=ओ), vriddhi (अ+ए=ऐ, अ+ओ=औ), yan (इ/उ/ऋ + swar = य्/व्/र्), ayadi (ए/ऐ/ओ/औ + स्वर = अय्/आय्/अव्/आव्).
3. **Vyanjan ya visarg juda hai?** Vyanjan sandhi (सत्+जन=सज्जन; आ+छादन=आच्छादन — छ→च्छ) ya visarg sandhi (निः+रोग=निरोग; मनः+हर=मनोहर).
4. **Jodo aur check karo:** joda hua shabd bolkar dekho — swabhavik lagna chahiye.

## Chapter 7 — Samas aur Samas-vigrah

**Official topic:** samas & samas-vigrah. **Bank MCQs:** `12_GENERAL_HINDI-003` (Rajkumar), `12_GENERAL_HINDI-004` (Mata-Pita), `12_GENERAL_HINDI-005` (Dashanan), `12_GENERAL_HINDI-006` (Tribhuvan), `12_GENERAL_HINDI-015` (Neelkanth — Bahuvrihi), `12_GENERAL_HINDI-017` (Yathashakti). **PYQs:** 2024-P1 Q15 (Samas – dhire-dhire); 2024-P1 Q27 (Samas-pad – advitiya); 2024-P2 Q22 (Matching – samas).

### A. VISUAL INTRODUCTION — do shabd, ek shabd, 6 tarike

  राजा + कुमार  = राजकुमार      (दूसरा पद प्रधान — तत्पुरुष)

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — do shabd ek ho jate hain

Sochiye aap kehte hain: "राजा का कुमार" — chaar shabd. Ab kehte hain: "राजकुमार" — ek shabd, wahi arth. Do ya adhik shabdon ke mel se ek naya shabd banna, jisme beech ki vibhakti (ka, ki, me) lupt ho jaye — isi ko **samas** kehte hain. Aur samas ko wapas kholna — "राजकुमार = राजा का कुमार" — **samas-vigrah** hai. Samas 6 prakar ke hain, aur har prakar ki pehchan ek hi sawaal se hoti hai: **kaun sa pad pradhana hai?** Dusra pad → tatpurush; visheshan+visheshya → karmadharaya; dono → dvandva; teesra arth → bahuvrihi; pehla pad sankhya → dvigu; pehla pad avyaya → avyayibhav.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — samas kyun banta hai?

- **Kyun banta hai?** Bhasha ko **chhota aur sundar** banane ke liye. "दश हैं आनन जिसके" kehne se "दशानन" kehna chhota aur kavyaatmak hai. Samas Sanskrit se aayi hui parampara hai — Hindi ne use apnaya hai.
- **Pradhanta kyun poochhi jati hai?** Kyunki samas ka prakar *arth* se tay hota hai, roop se nahi. 'Neelkanth' dekhne me karmadharaya lagta hai (neel + kanth = visheshan + visheshya), par arth teesre ki ore jata hai (Shiv) — isliye **Bahuvrihi** hai. Roop dhokha deta hai; arth sach batata hai.
- **Vigrah kyun?** Vigrah jaanchta hai ki aapne samas ko *samjha* hai — kholne wala hi sahi pehchan sakta hai.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — samas pehchanne ke 4 steps

1. **Vigrah karo:** shabd ko kholo ("राजकुमार = राजा का कुमार").
2. **Pradhanta poochho:** kaun sa pad pradhana hai — pehla, dusra, dono, ya koi teesra?
3. **Pehla pad check karo:** sankhya hai (त्रि) → dvigu; avyaya hai (यथा) → avyayibhav.
4. **Teesra arth check karo:** arth kisi teesre ki ore jata hai (दशानन → रावण) → **bahuvrihi** — ye sabse bada exam trap hai.

### A. VISUAL INTRODUCTION — shabd ke pehle aur peechhe lagne wale tukde

```
   UPSARG (pehle)              MOOL SHABD              PRATYAY (peechhe)
   ─────────────              ───────────             ─────────────
   प्र + हार  = प्रहार        हार (mool)             लघु + ता = लघुता
   अनु + भव  = अनुभव         भव (mool)              गुरु + त्व = गुरुत्व
   उत् + कर्ष = उत्कर्ष       कर्ष (mool)             कुरु + अ = कौरव
   निर् + भय = निर्भय        भय (mool)              पठ + आई = पढ़ाई

   UPSARG = shabd ke PEHLE judkar arth badle
   PRATYAY = shabd ke BAAD judkar naya shabd banaye
```

> **How to use this diagram:** Cover the middle column and join each pair yourself. Then cover the outer columns and split each joined word — pehle wala tukda upsarg hai ya peechhe wala pratyay?

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — shabd ke dono ore tukde judte hain

Sochiye 'हार' ek shabd hai — haar (defeat) ya haar (necklace). Ab iske **pehle** 'प्र' lagao — 'प्रहार' (hamla) — arth bilkul badal gaya. Iske **peechhe** kuchh lagao — 'हार' se 'हारना' — naya shabd ban gaya. Shabd ke pehle judne wala tukda **upsarg** hai (प्र, अनु, सम्, वि, निर्, उत्, सु...), aur shabd ke baad judne wala tukda **pratyay** hai (ता, त्व, ई, वाला, आई...). Upsarg shabd ka arth badalta hai; pratyay se naya shabd banta hai. Dono milkar Hindi ke shabdon ka bhandar badhate hain — aur exam me dono alag-alag poochhe jate hain.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — upsarg/pratyay kyun hain?

- **Kyun hain?** Kyunki bhasha ko naye shabd chahiye hote hain — aur har baar naya shabd banane se behtar hai purane shabd me tukda jod dena. 'Karu' se 'Kaurav' (vanshaj), 'laghu' se 'laghuta' (bhav) — ek tukde se naya arth. Ye bhasha ki **kifayat (economy)** hai.
- **Upsarg aur pratyay me antar kyun?** Upsarg **arth ki disha** badalta hai (हार → प्रहार — haar se hamla); pratyay **shabd ka prakar** badalta hai (लघु visheshan → लघुता sangya). Ek disha badalta hai, dusra prakar.
- **'Kaurav' me kya hua?** 'Kuru' + 'अ' (अण् pratyay) — 'उ' ki vriddhi hokar 'औ' bana (कु → कौ) — 'Kuru ke vanshaj' = Kaurav. Ye vriddhi sandhi ka niyam hai (Chapter 6) — upsarg/pratyay aur sandhi ek saath kaam karte hain.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — upsarg/pratyay pehchanne ke 3 steps

1. **Shabd ko todo:** pehle ya peechhe koi tukda juda hai? 'प्रहार' = प्र + हार.
2. **Tukda kahan hai?** Pehle → **upsarg**; baad me → **pratyay**.
3. **Arth check karo:** upsarg ne arth badla ya pratyay ne naya shabd banaya? 'Kaurav' = Kuru + अ (pratyay, peechhe) — 'Kaur' + 'Av' galat vichhed hai (bank 007).

> **How to use this diagram:** Cover the right column and say the synonyms/antonyms yourself. Then cover the left column and say the *type* from the pair. Type first, words second — that is the exam skill.

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — shabd ke chaar rishte

Sochiye shabdon ka ek parivar hai. Kuchh shabd **dost** hain — ek hi arth, alag-alag naam: जल, पानी, नीर, वारि — ye **paryayvachi** (synonyms) hain. Kuchh shabd **dushman** hain — bilkul ulta arth: सुख–दुख, जय–पराजय — ye **vilom** (antonyms) hain. Kuchh shabd **humshakal** hain — awaaz ek jaisi, arth alag, spelling bhi alag: दिन–दीन, अन्न–अन्य — ye **samshruti** (homophones) hain. Aur kuchh shabd **ek hi naam se kai kaam** karte hain: 'अर्थ' ka matlab dhan bhi hai, matlab bhi hai, uddeshya bhi — ye **anekarthak** hain. Exam me charon alag-alag poochhe jate hain — aur sabse bada trap hai inhe aapas me milana.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — ye chaar bhed kyun hain?

- **Paryayvachi kyun?** Kyunki bhasha me ek hi cheez ke kai naam bante hain — alag-alag kshetron, kaviyon aur samay se. 'Jal' Sanskrit se, 'paani' bolchal se — dono wahi cheez.
- **Vilom kyun?** Kyunki har gun ka ulta hota hai — sukh ka dukh, jeet ka haar. Vilom bhasha ko **virodh** batane ka tareeka hai.
- **Samshruti kyun mushkil hai?** Kyunki awaaz ek jaisi hai, isliye kaan dhokha deta hai — sirf **spelling aur arth** se antar pakda jata hai (दिन vs दीन — 'इ' ki matra ka antar).
- **Vilom ka roop kyun milna chahiye?** Kyunki vilom **usii prakar** ka hona chahiye: sangya ka vilom sangya (सुख–दुख), visheshan ka vilom visheshan (सुखी–दुखी). Roop na mile to vilom galat hai — ye exam ka trap hai.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — shabd-bhed pehchanne ke 3 steps

1. **Arth poochho:** ek hi arth → paryayvachi; ulta arth → vilom; ek shabd, kai arth → anekarthak; awaaz ek jaisi, arth alag → samshruti.
2. **Roop milao (vilom ke liye):** sangya ka vilom sangya, visheshan ka vilom visheshan — "सुख" ka vilom "दुख" hai, "दुखी" nahi.
3. **Spelling dekho (samshruti ke liye):** दिन–दीन, अन्न–अन्य — matra ka antar hi pehchan hai.

### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — ek shabd kyun banta hai?

### B. STORY-BASED EXPLANATION — bhasha ke rang

Sochiye aap kehte hain: "Mera beta mujhe bahut priya hai." — sahi baat hai, par bejaan hai. Ab kehte hain: "Mera beta meri **aankhon ka tara** hai." — wahi baat, par rang aa gaya! **Muhavare** bhasha ke rang hain: chhote se vakyaansh me badi bhavna. 'Aankhon ka tara' ka matlab aankh ka tara (pupil) nahi hai — matlab hai "bahut priya". ### C. WHY DOES IT HAPPEN? — muhavare kyun bante hain?

- **Kyun bante hain?** Kyunki seedhi baat me **bhavna** nahi hoti. "Bahut pareshan karna" kehne se 'naak me dam karna' zyada zor se kehta hai — bhavna shabd se badi hoti hai. Muhavare bhasha ko **jeevant** banate hain.
- **Arth shabdon se kyun nahi milta?** Kyunki muhavare ka arth **parampara (convention)** se milta hai, shabd ke arth se nahi. 'Aankhon ka tara' ka shabdarth "aankh ka tara" hai, par muhavara-arth "bahut priya" hai. Ye ratna nahi — **samajhna** hai: har muhavare ke peechhe ek chhoti tasveer hai (aankh ka tara = sabse keemti cheez).
- **Muhavara vs lokokti — antar kyun?** Muhavara **vakya ka hissa** hai ("usne meri naak me dam kar diya"); lokokti **khud ek vakya** hai ("जैसी करनी वैसी भरनी") — use kisi vakya me jodne ki zaroorat nahi.

### D. HOW DOES IT WORK? — muhavara/lokokti pehchanne ke 3 steps

1. **Arth poochho:** shabdarth se matlab milta hai ya nahi? Nahi → muhavara/lokokti ho sakta hai.
2. **Poora vakya hai ya hissa?** Hissa (vakya me judta hai) → **muhavara**. Khud poora vakya → **lokokti**.
3. **Tasveer dekho:** har muhavare ke peechhe ek tasveer hai — 'aankhon ka tara' = aankh ki sabse keemti cheez; 'andhe ki lathi' = andhe ka ekmatra sahara. Tasveer se arth yaad rakho.
